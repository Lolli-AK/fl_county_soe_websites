Find Precinct & Polling Place

To find your precinct and learn where you must vote on Election Day , follow these steps:

  1. Type in your house number in the following section. (Do not copy and paste, you must type the number in.)
  2. As you enter your street name, you will be prompted with valid street names found in Pinellas County. You do not need to enter the direction or street type. For example, if you live on 66th Street N, just type 66th.
  3. When the computer gives you street names, select yours from the options and the precinct information will appear below (scroll down for the information).
