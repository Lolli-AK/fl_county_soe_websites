Accuracy in Elections and Voter Services is our Commitment to Excellence
The Bay County Supervisor of Elections is committed to maintaining the highest standards of quality in the planning, preparation, and execution of all electoral processes. We ensure that elections are fair, transparent, and conducted in accordance with legal requirements, fostering trust and confidence within our community.
