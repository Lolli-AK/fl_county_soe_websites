Learning, Leading & Staying Prepared!
There’s always something new to learn when it comes to serving the public!
Yesterday, Supervisor of Elections Vicki Davis, Chief Deputy Kherri Anderson, Deputy of Voter Services Administrator Lisa Rodriguez, Deputy of Technology Administration Sam Wilton, and Deputy of Elections Finance & Compliance Christy Lopez attended “Surviving 1st Amendment Audits” training, sponsored by the City of Stuart and held at the Blake Library.
Thank you to instructor Leith Harrell for sharing valuable insight into First Amendment audits, public interactions, and best practices for navigating these situations while continuing to provide professional, respectful, and transparent service.
A big thank you to the City of Stuart for providing this informative training opportunity!
Our Elections Team is committed to continuous learning, staying prepared, and providing the best possible service to Martin County voters.
