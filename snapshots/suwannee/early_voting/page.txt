Vote Early
Identification Required to Vote Early
Any voter in the county may choose to cast their ballot during the early voting period. Be sure to bring your
valid photo and signature identification
with you when you present yourself to vote. Any voter who does not provide the proper identification will be offered a  Provisional Ballot.
