Thank you for visiting the Gilchrist County Supervisor of Elections website. This website provides the latest information to facilitate a smooth voting experience. You will find detailed election information and tools, including voter registration, vote-by-mail ballot requests, and Election Day voting.
It is an honor to serve as your Supervisor of Elections. If you need assistance, please contact our office.
-Lisa Darus
