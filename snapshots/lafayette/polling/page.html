Where Do I Vote?

To find out where you should go to vote on Election Day:
Enter your house number.
Enter your street name.

You don’t need to enter the direction (north, east, ect.) or the street type (Road, Avenue, etc.)
Example: If you live on East Bay Drive, just enter Bay.

Note:  Your information must be typed in. It can’t be copied and pasted from another source.

Select your street name from the list that appears.

Accessibility Statement

We are committed to keeping our site accessible to everyone. We welcome feedback on ways to improve the site’s accessibility.
