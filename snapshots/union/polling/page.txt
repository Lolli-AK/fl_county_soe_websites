Voting Location Finder
Download the
Union County Polling Places flyer
to see your voting location based on your precinct. If you do not know your precinct, enter your house number and street name in the appropriate fields below to locate your Election Day polling place.
Directions for Use
Enter your house number
Slowly type in your street name. You do not need to enter the direction or street type.
Example: If you live on East Kennedy Blvd., type in Kennedy.
Select your street name from the options that appear
Please note you cannot copy and paste the address. It must be slowly typed in.
