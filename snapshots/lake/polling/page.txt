Find My Precinct
Enter your house number and street name in the appropriate fields below to locate your Election Day polling place.
Directions for use:
Type in your house number.
Type in your street name. (You do not need to enter the direction (N, S, W, etc.) or street type (St., Ave., etc.))
Example: If you live on West Main St., type in Main. Select your street name from the options that appear.
