Okaloosa County, Florida

Supervisor of Elections

Paul Lux has lived in Okaloosa County since 1997. He joined the elections office in 1999 and served in various capacities until he was elected Supervisor of Elections in 2008.

During his time with the office, Paul helped keep Okaloosa County on the leading edge of the elections community, participating in equipment certification testing, voting equipment upgrades, and election audits and recounts in other counties.
