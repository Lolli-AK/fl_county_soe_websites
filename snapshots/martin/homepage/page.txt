History, Heritage & Elections at the Flagler!
Supervisor of Elections Vicki Davis had the honor of serving as the first speaker for the Speakers at the Flagler Program, presented by the Stuart Heritage Museum!
Mrs. Davis shared stories from her personal journey and Martin County roots while also giving attendees a look into the world of voting and elections and the important work happening at the Supervisor of Elections Office.
As part of the evening’s historical reflection, attendees also had the opportunity to see a Sample Ballot preserved from 1986, during the time Peggy Robbins served as Martin County’s Supervisor of Elections. This piece of election history offered a meaningful connection to the county’s past and the evolution of the elections process.
Joining Mrs. Davis for the evening were Chief Deputy Kherri Anderson, along with several familiar faces from our community. We were especially pleased to have Mr. Navitsky, former Superintendent of Schools, in attendance. Mr. Gary Maxwell is also pictured with Mrs. Davis.
It was a wonderful evening filled with local history, community connections, and conversations about elections!
A special thank you to the Stuart Heritage Museum for inviting Mrs. Davis to kick off the Speakers at the Flagler Program, and thank you to everyone who joined us for this special evening!

Lights, Camera… Elections!
We recently welcomed Martin County Television (MCTV) to the Supervisor of Elections Office for a special recorded tour!
From the spaces where our team works each day to the equipment and processes that help keep elections running smoothly, this upcoming feature will give Martin County residents a behind-the-scenes look at your Elections Office!
We’re excited to open our doors and give you a closer look at the work that happens beyond the ballot — and the people, preparation, and processes that help make every election possible.
Stay tuned! The tour will air on MCTV later this month, and we can’t wait for you to come along for the visit!

Labor Day Office Closure
In observance of Labor Day, the Martin County Supervisor of Elections Office will be closed on Monday, September 7.
Today, we celebrate the dedication and hard work of workers across our community and throughout our nation. We hope everyone enjoys a safe and relaxing Labor Day!
Our office will resume normal business hours on Tuesday, September 8.
Happy Labor Day from Supervisor of Elections Vicki Davis and your Martin County Elections Team!
