Online Voter Registration
RegisterToVoteFlorida.gov
To register to vote online, you must have a Florida Driver's License or Florida ID card and the last four digits of your Social Security Number. The Online Florida Voter Registration Application form can be used to submit a new registration or to change your existing registration information (i.e., party affiliation, address changes, name changes, etc.). If you are registering to vote in the State of Florida for the first time, you may be required to print, sign, and mail in your Voter Registration Application.
For more information on the online voter registration process, please see the Florida Division of Elections' Frequently Asked Questions:
Voter FAQs - English (PDF)
Voter FAQs - Spanish (PDF)

Voter Registration Application
Individuals can download a Voter Registration Application Form to register to vote or update their voter registration information, including their signature. To complete this form, you must provide your Florida Driver License Number, Florida Identification Card Number, or the last four digits of your Social Security Number.
