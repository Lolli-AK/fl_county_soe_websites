🇺🇸 **EARLY VOTING DOESN'T TAKE WEEKENDS OFF!** ❤️🤍💙
It's Sunday, and neither do we! 😊
Our doors are open, and we're here ready to welcome Jefferson County voters!
Whether you're heading home from church, finishing up your weekend errands, or just enjoying your Sunday, why not stop by and cast your ballot?
🗳️ **Early Voting is underway!**
📅 October 19–31, 2026
⏰ 8:00 AM – 5:00 PM DAILY (including weekends!)
📍 Jefferson County Supervisor of Elections Office
✅ Bring your photo and signature ID.
✅ Review your sample ballot before arriving.
✅ Don't forget to check BOTH sides of your ballot!
We're here until 5:00 today, and we'd love to see you!
🇺🇸 **Your Vote. Our
Priority.**
