Celebrating National Voter Registration Day with the Next Generation of Voters!
Our Elections Team had a great time celebrating National Voter Registration Day at Indian River State College’s Chastain Campus!
A big THANK YOU to all the students who stopped by to register or pre-register to vote! It was exciting to see so many students taking an important step toward making their voices heard.
A very special thank you to Professor Yankwitt for always collaborating with our office and bringing students over to register and pre-register. We truly appreciate your continued partnership and commitment to encouraging civic engagement among students!
Every registration is a step toward a more engaged community!
