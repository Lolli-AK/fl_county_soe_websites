Thank you for taking the time to visit your Calhoun County Supervisor of Elections website. You will be able to register to vote; request a mail ballot; locate your polling precinct; find early voting dates & times; and much more. It is our goal that this site encourages citizen participation, provide confidence and education to the election process.

All mail ballot requests expired after 2024. Request mail ballots now for elections through 2026.

PRIMARY ELECTION

  • Book Closing:  July 20
  • Vote-by-Mail (Deadline to request):  Aug. 6
  • Early Voting
    • SOE Office:  Aug. 3-15
      • Hours (M-F): 7 AM – 5 PM
      • (Sat & Sun): 8 AM – 4 PM
    • Shelton Park Library:  Aug. 8-15
      • Hours: 10 AM – 6 PM
  • Election Day – August 18, 2026

GENERAL ELECTION

  • Book Closing:  Oct. 5
  • Vote-by-Mail (Deadline to request):  Oct. 22
  • Early Voting:
    • SOE Office:  Oct. 19-31
      • Hours (M-F): 7 AM – 5 PM
      • (Sat & Sun): 8 AM – 4 PM
    • Shelton Park Library:  Oct. 24-31
      • Hours: 10 AM – 6 PM

Accessibility Statement

We are committed to keeping our site accessible to everyone. We welcome feedback on ways to improve the site’s accessibility .
