Early Voting

Early Voting in Calhoun County is open to all eligible register voters. Early Voting lets you avoid long lines, and vote when and where it is most convenient for you.

  • When is Early Voting Available?

Early Voting is available as part of each election here in Calhoun County. State law sets a minimum of eight days of Early Voting, but here in Calhoun County we offer additional days so that voters have more flexibility when casting their ballot.

  • Where Can I Vote Early?

There are two (2) Early Voting sites in Calhoun County. Voters can vote at either Early Voting sit in the county during voting hours.

Supervisor of Elections Office
20859 Central Ave East. Rm 117
Blountstown, FL 32424
————
Shelton Park Library
25008 NW SR 73
Altha, FL 32421
  • Who Can Vote Early?

Early Voting is open to all registered voters in Calhoun County.

If you are registered to vote in another Florida county, but you have moved to Calhoun County, we can update your address at the Early Voting site.

  • Do I need ID to Vote Early?

Yes, you mush have an ID to vote at an Early Voting site.

  • Can Anyone Enter an Early Voting Site?

The only people inside the Early Voting site during voting hours include:

  • Voters who are voting
  • Poll Workers
  • Persons under the care of a voter, such as a child
  • Persons helping a voter with a disability
  • Official poll watchers
  • Supervisor of Elections staff
  • Law enforcement/emergency service personnel, if requested by the Clerk
  • Can I Drop-Off my Vote-by-Mail Ballot at an Early Voting Site?

Yes, you can drop-off a Calhoun County Vote-by-Mail ballot at any Early Voting site in the county during voting hours. Each Voting site has a Secure Ballot Intake Station (f/k/a drop box) for Vote-by-Mail ballots.
