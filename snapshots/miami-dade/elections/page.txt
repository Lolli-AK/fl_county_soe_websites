Petition Amendments, Initiatives & Referendums
Citizens can petition to propose state amendments, as well as Miami-Dade and municipal
initiatives and referendums, to go up for a vote if certain requirements are met.
Voters with Special Needs
Voters with disabilities have multiple voting options that meet Americans with Disabilities
Act (ADA) standards.
Provisional Voting
A provisional ballot shall be issued to a person who may not meet voter requirements.
The ballot is then reviewed by the Canvassing Board.
Overseas and Military Voters
Overseas and military voters who are Miami-Dade County residents can access resources
to participate in local elections.
Restoration of Voting Rights
If you are convicted of a felony, you lose the right to vote in Florida until your
voting rights are restored.

Redistricting
Every 10 years, the Florida Legislature and certain local jurisdictions redraw districts
from which Florida voters elect local, state and federal government officials.
Reprecincting
After redistricting, the Supervisor of Elections updates the county’s voting precincts
to conform the precinct boundaries to the new district boundaries.

Voter Information
Our online Voter Information tool allows you to review your current voting districts (click on “View Office Holders”), your voter information (address, political party, polling location, etc.) request a mail ballot, check the status of your mail ballot, and view your customized sample ballot for the upcoming election.
You can also make changes to your voter information, check your voter activity over the last year, and more.
To learn more, check out our
Voter Information Guide.
