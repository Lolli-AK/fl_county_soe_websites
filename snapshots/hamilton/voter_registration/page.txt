Register to Vote Online
You can register online at the Florida Department of State’s website, click below.
Mail in Voter Registration
Complete the following Florida Voter Registration Application. Then print it, sign your name, and mail the application to our office at 1153 US Hwy 41 NW, Suite 1, Jasper, FL 32052.

English
Español
Contact Us
Contact the Hamilton County Supervisor of Elections office at
(386) 792-1426
, and we will mail you a Florida Voter Registration Application. You can also register in person at the Hamilton County Supervisor of Elections office or at one of our county libraries.
Military or Overseas U.S. Citizen:
If you are a military or overseas U.S. citizen, you may register to vote and request a vote-by-mail ballot at the same time by using the
Federal Post Card Application
(FPCA). Go to the Division of Elections web page on
Military and Overseas Voting
for further details.
Voter Registration Deadline
The deadline to register to vote for an upcoming election is 29 days before the election. The deadline to submit a party change before a primary election is also 29 days before that election. For more information, visit the
Division of Elections
website.
