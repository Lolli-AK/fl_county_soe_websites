Voter Registration

As American citizens, the right to vote is one of our most important civic rights.

You have the unique opportunity to participate in free, fair, and frequent elections.  Everyone who is eligible should take advantage of having their voice heard by exercising their right to vote.

Be a part of voter participation history and make a commitment to be engaged in the elections process. Be an example to others, especially to those young future voters.

No matter what you believe or whom you support, be a voter!
