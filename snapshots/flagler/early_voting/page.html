Early Voting

Vote Early in Flagler County

Recent turnout shows more voters are choosing to vote early!

Any registered and qualified voter who is eligible to vote in a countywide election may vote in person at an early voting site.

Voters who have requested a mail ballot may return their ballot at a drop box at one of our early voting sites, during the schedule below. More information about mail ballot drop boxes is available at the bottom of this page.

According to Florida law, every voter must present a Florida driver license, a Florida identification card, or another form of acceptable picture and signature identification in order to vote. If you do not present the required identification or if your eligibility cannot be determined, you will only be permitted to vote a provisional ballot.

Don't forget your ID!

Mail Ballot Drop Boxes

Voted mail ballots may be returned by using one of our secure ballot intake stations (drop boxes) during an election cycle. These drop boxes will be monitored and available at the Elections Office and all early voting sites during the early voting period. Voters may deposit their mail ballots in these drop boxes at any time the early voting sites are open for voting.

You may choose to return your ballot directly to the Elections Office, or one of our early voting sites:

Elections Office Drop Box

Elections Office Drop Box

This secure ballot intake station (drop box) is not available after office hours or on weekends, except during the early voting period. You may use this drop box to return any election-related paperwork and voted mail ballots during an election cycle.

Elections Office Drop Box Schedule

Available during office hours and extended office hours until 6:00 PM during the early voting period.

Office hours:
Monday through Friday from 8:30 AM – 4:30 PM

Any person may not return more than two Vote-By-Mail ballots per election in addition to his or her own ballot, or a ballot belonging to an immediate family member. The term “immediate family” means a person’s spouse or the parent, child, grandparent, grandchild, or sibling of the person or the person’s spouse. Reference: Section 104.0616, F.S.

Early Voting Drop Box Locations

Early Voting Drop Boxes

These secure ballot intake stations (drop boxes) are available at all of our early voting sites during early voting hours. Any voter can return their mail ballot at an early voting site. The drop box is located in or near the lobby area at these locations. Ballots returned in these drop boxes during early voting are delivered to the Elections Office the same day.
