Where Do I Vote
Florida law requires that you vote in the precinct where you live. Your voter information card shows the precinct, polling place, and voting districts for your address. If you move within the county, you must vote in your new precinct. Do not return to your old precinct to vote.
Important Reminder!
It is a felony to vote in a precinct where you do not live on election day.
If you are unsure of your correct precinct, you can find it by using the form below. If you already know your precinct number, browse the list of precincts below for location information. You can also always call our office at 863-534-5888. We will be pleased to provide you with your precinct number and location.
