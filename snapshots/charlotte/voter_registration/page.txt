How To Register to Vote
To register to vote in Florida, you must complete a
Voter Registration Application
.
Where to Get a Voter Registration Application
Voter Registration Applications are available at a variety of convenient locations, including your local Supervisor of Elections office, the Florida Division of Elections, driver's license offices, state agencies that provide public assistance, agencies serving persons with disabilities, public libraries, and many other public locations.
Additionally, you will be offered the opportunity to register or update your voter information when applying for or renewing your Florida driver's license, applying for public assistance, or receiving services from a state agency that serves individuals with disabilities.
What You'll Need to Register
A Voter Registration Application is considered complete for new registrations if it includes:
Your full name
Your legal residential address
Your date of birth
Identification number
:
Florida driver's license
or
Florida state ID number
If you do not have either, provide the
last four digits of your Social Security number
If you have not been issued a Florida driver's license, a Florida identification card, or a Social Security number, you must indicate "none" in the box provided for this information.
Check the box affirming you are a
citizen of the United States
Check the box affirming that you
have not been convicted of a felony
, or if convicted, that your
civil rights have been restored
Check the box affirming that you
have not been adjudicated mentally incapacitated
with respect to voting, or if so, that your
right to vote has been restored
Sign the oath
on the application, affirming under penalty of perjury that the information provided is true
Note
: Power of attorney signatures are not accepted.
Political Party Affiliation
If you
do not select
a political party, your registration will be recorded as
No Party Affiliation (NPA)
