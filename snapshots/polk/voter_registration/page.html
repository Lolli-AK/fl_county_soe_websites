Registration Form Locations

You can call the Supervisor of Elections Office or email us to have a form mailed to you. Click here to download a registration application form for a printable paper application.

If you need to obtain an original voter registration application, you can find one at many different locations throughout Polk County, including: the Supervisor of Elections offices, all Department of Motor Vehicles offices, all Public Assistance offices, public libraries, city halls, retirement centers, schools, and major businesses.
