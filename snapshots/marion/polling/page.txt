Find My Voting Location
The Election Center's online Precinct Finder will allow you to use your residential address to find your Voting Location.
To find your voting location, you will need to enter the information from your
residential address
. Please note that you cannot copy and paste the address. It must be typed in.
Example Address:
981 NE 16th Street
Enter your house number.
Example: 981
Type in your street name.
Example: 16th
Select your street name from the options that appear.
