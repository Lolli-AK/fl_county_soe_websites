📬 **CHECK YOUR MAILBOX!**
The initial mailing of **Vote-by-Mail ballots for the 2026 General Election** went out on **September 24!** 🇺🇸🗳️
If you’ve already requested a Vote-by-Mail ballot, **be on the lookout — it should be arriving soon!**
Once your ballot arrives, take your time, follow the instructions carefully, and remember to **sign the Voter’s Certificate on the return envelope** before returning your ballot.
Haven’t requested your Vote-by-Mail ballot yet? The deadline to request that a ballot be mailed to you is **Thursday, October 22 at 5:00 p.m.**
📬 **Be Informed. Be Prepared. Be Election Ready!**
