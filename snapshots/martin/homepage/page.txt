Tracking your Vote-by-Mail ballot has never been easier!
Once you have signed up for BallotTrax alerts, you’ll receive text or email notifications when we mail, receive, and accept your ballot.
If your ballot is undeliverable, missing your signature, or the signature does not match the signature we have on record, you’ll be notified so you can make any required corrections.
Visit MartinVotes.gov, select BallotTrax, and sign up today for text or email ballot status updates.
