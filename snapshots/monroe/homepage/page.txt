Voter Registration
If you would like to register to vote in Monroe County, or change or update your current registration, you can do that online here:
or by downloading, completing and returning this registration form
(
English
or
Español
).
You can also come into one of our offices to get a form to complete in-office or at home.
Click
here
for more information about registering to vote.
If you would like to check the status of your registration or your Vote-by-Mail Ballot Request,
CLICK HERE
and enter the information as instructed.

Welcome
We are happy to welcome all visitors to our website, especially those of you who are Monroe County voters. This site is primarily for you.
If you have arrived here - at our new site - by entering our old site address or through an old bookmark, please update your bookmarks to reflect our new site address:
www.votemonroeflkeys.gov
Inside you'll find important information about our offices, how to
register to vote
, the
voting process
, and much more.
