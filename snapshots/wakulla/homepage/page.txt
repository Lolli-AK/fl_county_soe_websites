Thank you to our Poll Workers
for the 2026 Elections
We celebrate our true Election Heroes, our poll workers!
Thank you for your long hours, dedication, patience, and commitment to serving our community and protecting the integrity of our elections.
From early morning setup to the final ballot cast, your hard work helps ensure every voter has the opportunity to make their voice heard.
We are incredibly grateful for your service, professionalism, and unwavering commitment to democracy. Thank you for making Election Day a success!
You are the backbone of our elections, and we appreciate all that you do.
Joe Morgan and staff
Florida Driver License Changes: What to Know
As of July 1, 2024, new Florida Driver License or state ID cards are issued new numbers.
Why is that important?
Your driver license is one of they ways we verify voters when they vote in person, by mail or submit a petition.
What should you do?
When renewing or updating your driver license, select the option to update your voter record.  When you do that, your voter record will be updated with your new number.
Didn’t opt to update?  What now?
Download the voter registration form below or call us at 850.926.7575 for assistance.
