Early Voting
Any voter in the county may choose to cast their ballot during the early voting period. Be sure to bring your valid photo and signature identification with you when you present yourself to vote.
Early Voting is held at
Dixie County Supervisor of Elections Office
229 NE 351 HWY, Suite A
Cross City, FL 32628
2026 Election Early Voting Dates & Times
Primary Election:
Saturday August 8, 2026 – Saturday August 15, 2026
Monday – Saturdays 8:00 a.m. to 6:00 p.m.
Sunday 8:00 a.m. to 4:00 p.m.
General Election:
Saturday October 22, 2026 – Saturday October 31, 2026
Monday – Saturdays 8:00 a.m. to 6:00 p.m.
Sunday 8:00 a.m. to 4:00 p.m.
Identification Required to Vote
Florida Statute 101.043 requires photo and signature identification at the polling place
Accepted documents are
Florida Driver’s License
Florida Identification Card issued by the DHSMV
United States Passport
Debit or Credit Card
Military Identification
Student Identification
Retirement Center Identification
Neighborhood Association Identification
Public Assistance Identification
Veteran’s Health Identification Card
Concealed Weapon or Firearm Permit
Employee ID card issued by any branch, department, agency, or entity of the Federal Government, the state, a county, or a municipality
Any combination of these documents may be presented to fulfill the photo and signature requirements.
Any voter who does not provide the proper identification will be offered a Provisional Ballot.
