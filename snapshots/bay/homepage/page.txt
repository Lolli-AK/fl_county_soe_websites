🗳️ Election Day may be over, but our work continues.
Since the polls closed, your Bay County Supervisor of Elections team has been hard at work completing the important steps that help ensure our election results are accurate, secure and transparent.
Our staff has been busy:
🔹 Scanning paper ballots into our audit system
🔹 Auditing voting history
🔹 Safely picking up and securing election equipment
🔹 Preparing for certification and our paper ballot audit
Monday at 8:30 AM, the Bay County Canvassing Board will meet to certify the Primary Election results and conduct an audit of 100% of our paper ballots. Auditing every paper ballot provides another important check of our election results—and another opportunity to demonstrate the accuracy and transparency of the process.
From the first ballot cast to final certification, we're committed to getting it right.

Accuracy in Elections and Voter Services is our Commitment to Excellence
The Bay County Supervisor of Elections is committed to maintaining the highest standards of quality in the planning, preparation, and execution of all electoral processes. We ensure that elections are fair, transparent, and conducted in accordance with legal requirements, fostering trust and confidence within our community.
