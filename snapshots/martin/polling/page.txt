2 days ago
The Doors Were Open, and Elections Were on the Agenda!
We were pleased to welcome the Women’s Democratic Group of Martin County to the Martin County Elections Center for a tour with Supervisor of Elections Vicki Davis!
During their visit, the group had the opportunity to explore the Elections Center, learn more about voting equipment and election operations, and hear directly from Mrs. Davis about the work that goes into serving Martin County voters.
The visit was filled with great questions, engaging conversation, and plenty of election information!
Thank you to the Women’s Democratic Group of Martin County for spending time with us and learning more about the election process. We always enjoy opening our doors and connecting with members of our community!

Celebrating National Voter Registration Day with the Next Generation of Voters!
Our Elections Team had a great time celebrating National Voter Registration Day at Indian River State College’s Chastain Campus!
A big THANK YOU to all the students who stopped by to register or pre-register to vote! It was exciting to see so many students taking an important step toward making their voices heard.
A very special thank you to Professor Yankwitt for always collaborating with our office and bringing students over to register and pre-register. We truly appreciate your continued partnership and commitment to encouraging civic engagement among students!
Every registration is a step toward a more engaged community!
