Election Dates
Polls open at 7 a.m. and close at 7 p.m. on Election Day!
Upcoming Elections 2026
March 10, 2026 – Trailer Estates Park & Recreation District Election
Voter Registration Deadline:
“Owner of Record” 30 days prior to election (02/08/2026)
Deadline to request a Vote by Mail ballot to be mailed: 5:00 pm Thursday, February 26, 2026
Deadline to return Vote by Mail ballot: Monday, March 9, 2026 by 5:00 pm
Election day: Tuesday, March 10, 2026  Polls open 12:00 pm – Polls close 8:00 pm
March 10, 2026 – West Manatee Fire & Rescue District Special Election
Voter Registration Deadline:
Monday, February 9, 2026
Deadline to request a Vote by Mail ballot to be mailed: 5:00 pm Thursday, February 26, 2026
Deadline to return Vote by Mail ballot: Tuesday, March 10, 2026 by 7:00 pm
Election day: Tuesday, March 10, 2026  Polls open 7:00 am – Polls close 7:00 pm
August 18, 2026 – Primary Election
Voter Registration Deadline:
Monday, July 20, 2026
Deadline to request a Vote by Mail ballot to be mailed: 5:00 pm Thursday, August 6, 2026
Deadline to return Vote by Mail ballot: Tuesday, August 18, 2026 by 7:00 pm
Early Voting: Saturday, August 8, 2026 through Sunday, August 16, 2026
8:30 am to 6:00 pm daily
Election day: Tuesday, August 18, 2026  Polls open 7:00 am – Polls close 7:00 pm
November 3, 2026 – General Election
(Including City of Anna Maria, City of Bradenton, City of Bradenton Beach,
City of Holmes Beach, and City of Palmetto)
Voter Registration Deadline:
Monday, October 5, 2026
Deadline to request a Vote by Mail ballot to be mailed: 5:00 pm Thursday, October 22, 2026
Deadline to return Vote by Mail ballot: Tuesday, November 3, 2026 by 7:00 pm
Early Voting: Monday, October 19, 2026 through Sunday, November 1, 2026
8:30 am to 6:00 pm daily
Election day: Tuesday, November 3, 2026  Polls open 7:00 am – Polls close 7:00 pm
