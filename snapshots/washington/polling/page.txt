Municipalities within Washington County
Washington County is home to several incorporated municipalities, each with its own local government offices and contact information listed here for your convenience. Residents can find key addresses and phone numbers for the towns of Caryville, Chipley, Ebro, Vernon, and Wausau. Additionally, the county includes several unincorporated communities that contribute to the diverse character of the area. For more information or assistance, please reach out to the appropriate municipal office using the contacts provided.
