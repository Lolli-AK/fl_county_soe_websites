Important Vote-by-Mail Information
2026 General Election Vote-by-Mail Ballot Deadlines
The deadline for the Supervisor of Elections to receive a voted vote-by-mail ballot is November 3, 2026, at 7 p.m. ET
The deadline for the Supervisor of Elections to receive a voted vote-by-mail from a domestic uniformed services member or Merchant Marine or dependent of such member is November 3, 2026, at 7 p.m. ET
The 10-day deadline extension for overseas voted ballots to be returned by mail is November 13, 2026.
Provisional and Signature Cure Affidavit Deadlines
The deadline to cure a provisional ballot is November 5, 2026, at 5 p.m. ET
The deadline to cure a vote-by-mail ballot signature is November 5, 2026, at 5 p.m. ET
