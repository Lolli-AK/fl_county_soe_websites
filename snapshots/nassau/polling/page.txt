Where Do I Vote?
Precinct Locator
The precinct you live in determines which races will be on your ballot and where you vote on Election Day. Use the precinct finder on this page to find your precinct number, information about your precinct, and the office holders who represent voters in your precinct. You can also click on the link in the search tool below to see a list of all precincts, and their current voting locations.
To find your precinct and learn where you must vote on Election Day, follow these steps:
Type in your house number below. (Do not copy and paste, you must type the number in.)
As you enter your street name, you will be prompted with valid street names found in Nassau County. You do not need to enter the direction or street type. For example, if you live on Chester Road, just type Chester.
When the computer gives you street names, select yours from the options and the precinct information will appear below (scroll down for the information).
Official Election Mail will contain the following symbol.
