Update Your Signature
Posted November 6, 2023 | Revised November 8, 2023
Has your signature changed over the years? Now is the time to update the signature on your voter record with the Lee County Supervisor of Elections. It is crucial that your voter record contain your current signature.
Having your signature up to date may seem like a minor thing. However, it can determine whether your vote or signature will be counted when you choose to vote by mail, cast a Provisional Ballot during Early Voting or on Election Day, or sign a petition. Florida's law requires that the signature on your Vote-by-Mail ballot, Provisional Ballot voter's certificate, or signed petition be compared to the signature on your voter record.
All signature updates for use in verifying vote-by-mail voter certificates, Provisional Ballot voter certificates, or petitions must be received by our office before we receive the voter's ballot. In the case of a Provisional Ballot, before the ballot is cast, or in the case of a petition, before the petition is submitted for signature verification.
To update your signature, you must complete a Florida Voter Registration Application.
You may obtain an application below, at any of our
office locations,
or you can
call the office
to request that an application be mailed to you.
Florida Voter Registration Application (English PDF)
Florida Voter Registration Application (Spanish PDF)
The completed, original application must be returned by mail or in person at any of
our office locations
.
Visit the Voter Resources section of this site for more information on
updating your signature or voter registration information
.
Updating your Florida Driver's License or ID Card Number
Have you recently updated or been issued a new Florida driver's license or ID card? Due to changes implemented to the numbering system
by the Department of Highway Safety and Motor Vehicles (DHSMV)
, your new driver's license or identification card number may be different from the one on your voter registration record. I
t’s important to update your voter registration information with your new number. This identification number helps us verify your identity when processing your request for a vote-by-mail ballot or an address change.
To update your voter registration with your new Florida driver’s license or ID card number, you may:
Call us at (239) LEE-VOTE (533-8683)
Visit
RegisterToVoteFlorida.gov
to update your record.
Complete and submit a Florida Voter Registration Application
Florida Voter Registration Application (English PDF)
Florida Voter Registration Application (Spanish PDF)
Update your registration when you update your driver’s license or ID card at the DMV.
