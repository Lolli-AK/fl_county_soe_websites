Military and Overseas Voting

Voting for Military and Overseas Voters

The Uniformed and Overseas Citizens Absentee Voting Act ( UOCAVA ) protects the voting rights of:

  • Active-duty members and eligible dependents of the Uniformed Services or Merchant Marines living outside their voting jurisdiction
  • U.S. citizens living outside the country

Active-duty members and eligible dependents of the Uniformed Services or Merchant Marines living in their voting jurisdiction are not covered under the Uniformed and Overseas Citizens Absentee Voting Act ( UOCAVA ).

Submit a completed Federal Post Card Application, Election Forms and Materials , containing your original signature in order to:

  • Register to vote
  • Update voter registration information
  • Request vote-by-mail ballots


Please include your email address and updated contact information, including your mailing address, when submitting a Federal Post Card Application. The Federal Post Card Application Instructions (PDF) provides more information on completing the application.

Important Deadlines

  • Using the Federal Post Card Application to register to vote?
  • Your completed form must be received no later than 29 days before the election .
  • Using the Federal Post Card Application to request a vote-by-mail ballot?
  • Your completed form must be received no later than 12 days before the election .

Returning Your Ballot

Ballots must be in our office no later than 7 pm on Election Day .
Ballots may be returned to our office by one of the following methods:

By Mail

Lake County Supervisor of Elections
P.O. Box 457
Tavares, Florida 32778

Courier

Lake County Supervisor of Elections Office
1898 E Burleigh Boulevard
Tavares, Florida 32778

  • If you received your ballot by email, you can find additional return information through these resources:
    • Overseas Military and Civilian Voters (PDF)
    • Absent Stateside Military Voters (PDF)

Note:

  • If you or your dependent has been discharged or separated from the Uniformed Services or Merchant Marines, has returned from military deployment or activation, or has separated from employment outside the U.S. after book closing, you may register to vote until 5 pm on the Friday before an election. Sufficient documentation must be provided to show you qualify for late registration.
  • Overseas voters have until noon on the 10 th day after the election to return their ballots for any Presidential Preference Primary or General Election if it is postmarked or dated on or before the date of the election. Overseas voters may also return their ballots via fax to 352-253-1422 .

The Federal Write-In Absentee Ballot ( FWAB ) serves as an emergency backup ballot for all Uniformed and Overseas Citizens Absentee Voting Act
( UOCAVA ) voters who have requested a vote-by-mail ballot but have not received it. Use the Federal Write-In Absentee Ballot Instructions (PDF) for information on completing the Federal Write-In Absentee Ballot (FWAB).

The Federal Voting Assistance Program ( FVAP ) works to ensure service members, their eligible dependents, and overseas citizens are aware of their right to vote and have the tools and resources to successfully do so from anywhere in the world. To learn more about the Federal Voting Assistance Program ( FVAP ), download necessary forms, or view the online Voting Assistance Guide, visit Federal Voting Assistance Program .
