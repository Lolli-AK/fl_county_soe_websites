2026 General Election Post-Election Audit Complete! ✔️
The Jefferson County Canvassing Board has officially completed the post-election audit of the November 3, 2026, General Election.
The audit included 100% of the ballots cast, encompassing all 16 precincts and all races.
The audit verified the accuracy and reliability of our voting system, further demonstrating our commitment to transparency, accountability, and maintaining public confidence in the election process.
We are proud to serve the voters of Jefferson County and remain committed to conducting secure, accurate, and transparent elections.
