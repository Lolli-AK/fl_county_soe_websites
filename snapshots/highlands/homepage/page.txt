Serving Highlands County Voters
★
A Trusted Resource for Highlands County Voters
This website is provided as a public service by the Highlands County Supervisor of Elections. Our mission is to ensure that every eligible voter in Highlands County has access to accurate, timely, and nonpartisan election information. We are committed to upholding the highest standards of transparency, security, and integrity in every step of the electoral process.
We strive to empower voters with the tools and resources they need to participate confidently in local, state, and federal elections. Whether you're registering to vote, requesting a mail ballot, finding your polling place, or viewing a sample ballot — our goal is to make the process clear, convenient, and secure.
We welcome your feedback and are honored to serve the voters of Highlands County.

Petition Status Report
Florida citizens have the right to propose amendments to the Florida Constitution through an initiative petition process. Highlands County Supervisor of Elections verifies thousands of initiative petitions each year signed by voters in Highlands County. Sponsors of these initiatives must secure the required number of valid signatures in order for their initiative to be placed on a General Election ballot.
Pursuant to Florida Statute 100.371(11)(b), Highlands County's actual cost of signature verification of an initiative petition is $4.78 as of March 1, 2026.
