Book Closing
According to Florida State Statute, all party changes and new registrations for the 2026 Primary Election must be completed by July 20, 2026.
Vote By Mail
According to Florida State Statute, all Vote by Mail requests regarding the 2026 Primary Election must be made no later than 5pm on August 6th, 2026.
Early Vote
Early voting will be held at our new location from 8:30am to 6:00pm on August 8th, 2026. It will carry through the week until August 15, 2026.
Election Day
On August 18, 2026, all polling locations will be open. Our Election Workers will be available to assist you at your assigned precinct from 7am-7pm.
