TODAY IS THE FINAL DAY OF EARLY VOTING!
Jefferson County voters, if Early Voting is part of your voting plan, today is the day!
Early Voting for the August 18, 2026 Primary Election ends TODAY at 5:00 p.m.
📍 Jefferson County Supervisor of Elections Office
⏰ 8:00 a.m. – 5:00 p.m. TODAY
Our staff and poll workers are here and ready to welcome you!
If you don't vote early today, your next opportunity to cast your ballot will be on Election Day — Tuesday, August 18, when you must vote at your assigned polling place.
Not sure where you vote on Election Day? Visit
JeffersonVotesFL.gov
or contact our office at 850-997-3348.
It's been a wonderful Early Voting period, Jefferson County. Let's finish strong!
We look forward to serving you!
