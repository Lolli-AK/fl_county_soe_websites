Voter Information Lookup

This form below enables a voter to verify his or her registration status in Holmes County.  In addition, this search tool provides a wealth of voter-specific registration information.  If you want to check to see if you are registered to vote anywhere in Florida, go to the Florida Division of Elections Check Your Voter Status page.

Complete the form below to:

  1. Review or update your voter registration information
  2. Locate your Election Day polling location
  3. Look up your Florida Voter Registration Number
  4. See your sample ballot(s)
  5. See elected officials specific to your address
  6. Look up future elections
  7. Review your voting activity for the past 12 months
  8. Check the status of your mail (absentee) ballot.

If you are not registered to vote, please visit the Register to Vote page.

If you need to check on the status of a provisional ballot, please visit the provisional ballot page.

Check Registration Status

Since 2006, Florida has utilized a statewide computerized voter registration list called the Florida Voter Registration System (FVRS). Instead of registering to vote at just the county level, voters now register at the state level and are issued a unique state voter ID number. This has also made moving your registration from one county in Florida to another as simple as updating your address, rather than having to re-register to vote. Moving between counties in Florida is considered an address change, not subject to the 29 day book closing deadline, which applies to persons registering for the first time in Florida.


If you would like to check to see if you are registered anywhere in the state of Florida, you can use the search page Florida Statewide Voter Look-up
