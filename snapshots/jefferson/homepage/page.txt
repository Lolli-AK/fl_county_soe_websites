Jefferson County Supervisor of Elections - Michelle Milligan
posted 14 hours ago
🗳️❤️ **A Very Special Early Voting Moment!**
We had an exciting moment at the Jefferson County Supervisor of Elections Office today!
A voter who is visually impaired used the **accessible voting option** on our voting equipment to independently cast her ballot.
What made the moment even more special? A few years ago, she was the **first voter in Jefferson County to use this accessible option**, and today she had the opportunity to use it again right here during Early Voting!
She was excited—and needless to say, **so were we!** ❤️
After casting her ballot, she told us how easy the process was and even encouraged us to share her experience so that other voters who may need accessible voting assistance will know that this option is available to them.
Our goal is to ensure that **every eligible voter has the opportunity to cast their ballot privately, independently, and with
confidence.**
If you or someone you know has questions about accessible voting options, please contact our office at **850-997-3348**. We are always happy to explain the options available and assist in making your voting experience as comfortable and accessible as possible.
Moments like this are what it's all about. 🗳️🇺🇸
