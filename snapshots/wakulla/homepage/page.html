Special THANK YOU to our team of Poll Workers!

We would like to recognize the service provided to our county by the team of citizens that dedicated their time to be a poll worker.

We hold with gratitude, their devotion, commitment, and engagement to this critically important role in our American democracy.

Our poll workers are the unsung heroes who help all eligible voters cast a ballot.

They deserve our recognition, and we thank each and every one of our poll workers for the great work they do.

They are your neighbors, your family, your friends, and your community members.

Thank you.

In Person Voting ID Requirements

for the 2026 Elections

Do I need ID to vote?

Yes, you must bring ID to vote in person.  IDs must be valid and not expired.  Acceptable IDs include:

  • Florida Driver License
  • Florida Identification Card (issued by the Florida Department of Highway Safety and Motor Vehicles)
  • U.S. Passport
  • Debit or Credit Card
  • Military ID
  • Student ID
  • Retirement Center ID
  • Neighborhood Association ID
  • Public Assistance ID
  • Veteran Health ID (issued by the U.S. Department of Veterans Affairs)
  • A Concealed Weapons/Firearm License (issued pursuant to s.790.06, F.S.)
  • Employee ID issued by any branch, department, agency, or entity of the federal government, the state, a county, or a municipality.

Florida Driver License Changes: What to Know

As of July 1, 2024, new Florida Driver License or state ID cards are issued new numbers.

Why is that important?

Your driver license is one of they ways we verify voters when they vote in person, by mail or submit a petition.

What should you do?

When renewing or updating your driver license, select the option to update your voter record.  When you do that, your voter record will be updated with your new number.

Didn’t opt to update?  What now?

Download the voter registration form below or call us at 850.926.7575 for assistance.
