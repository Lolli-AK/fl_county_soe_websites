Early Voting
Monroe County Supervisor of Elections
Early Voting
Early Voting is when a voter votes a ballot prior to Election Day at one of the Elections offices or at one of the Designated Early Voting Locations and deposits it in the Ballot tabulator machine. The Early Voting tabulators are the same as the equipment used on Election Day.
When is Early Voting?
2026
Primary Election Early Voting
when:     8/3 -
8/15/2026    8:30am - 5:00pm
General Election Early Voting
when:    10/19 - 10/31/2026    8:30am - 5:00pm
For elections that contains state or federal races
:
Early voting begins 15 days prior to a county election.
The last day of Early Voting is the Saturday prior to the election.
Early voting will be everyday for those 13 days including two Saturdays and one Sunday.
Early Voting periods that contain only local races may differ. Per
FS §101.657(1)(d)
The supervisor of elections may provide early voting for elections that are not held in conjunction with a state or federal election. However, the supervisor has the discretion to determine the hours of operation of early voting sites in those elections.
How do I vote early?
Go to any of the Early Voting Locations during the Early Voting dates and times
Present your valid ID(s) (
List of Valid IDs
)
Receive and vote your ballot
Cast the ballot in the Ballot Tabulator Machine
Get your "
I Voted
" sticker and wear it proudly!
