Early Voting
2026 Primary Election Early Voting Dates
Saturday, August 8, 2026 - Saturday, August 15, 2026
Early Voting sites will be open:
Monday - Friday - 8:00 a.m. - 6:00 p.m.
Saturday - Sunday - 8:00 a.m. - 4:00 p.m.
2026 General Election Early Voting Dates
Monday, October 19, 2026 - Saturday, October 31, 2026
Early Voting sites will be open:
Monday - Friday - 8:00 a.m. - 6:00 p.m.
Saturday - Sunday - 8:00 a.m. - 4:00 p.m.
Voters may vote at any of Brevard's Early Voting sites regardless of their residential address. On Election Day, voters can only vote at their precinct specific polling location.
Don't know your precinct? Visit
Find Your Precinct
or refer to your Voter Information Card.
Secure Ballot Intake Stations are available during early voting hours for voters wishing to drop off their voted mail ballots. Voters can return their voted mail ballot to any of Brevard's Early Voting locations, or any of the
four administrative offices
.
