Early Voting

Early voting is conducted in the Supervisor of Elections Office. Our office is located at 32 N 5th Steet, Suite A, Macclenny FL 32063. Voting procedures are the same as on Election Day. Please remember to bring a valid photo and signature ID when coming in to early vote.

Example of valid photo and signature ID include:

  • - Florida Driver's License
  • - Florida ID Card issued by the DHSMV
  • - United States Passport
  • - Debit or Credit Card
  • - Military ID
  • - Student ID
  • - Public Assistance ID
  • - Retirement Center ID
