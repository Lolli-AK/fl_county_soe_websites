Voting Location Finder

Not sure which precinct is yours? Use the Votin g Location Finder to find your polling place:

  1. Enter your house number.
  2. Type in your street name. You do not need to enter the direction or street type. Example: If you live on East Kennedy Blvd., type in Kennedy.
  3. Select your street name from the options that appear.
  4. Click the submit button.
