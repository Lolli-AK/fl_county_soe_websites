🗳️ Early Voting Begins Today!
Today is the first day of Early Voting for the 2026 Primary Election, and we're ready to welcome voters!
Early Voting is available August 3–15, 8am to 5pm, daily at the Jefferson County Supervisor of Elections Office. If you're ready to cast your ballot, stop by at a time that's convenient for you.
If you have any questions, need to check your voter information, or would like assistance, we're here to help.
