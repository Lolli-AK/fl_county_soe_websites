Using This Tool
Enter your house number in the
House Number
field. Then, begin typing your street name in the
Street Name
field. Your street name will automatically appear as you type. Select the correct street from the drop-down menu, and your information will appear. There is no enter button.
This secure Find Your Polling Place tool is provided in partnership with our official elections software vendor, VR Systems, and is an authorized service of the Orange County Supervisor of Elections.
