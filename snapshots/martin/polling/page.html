It’s National Voter Education Week!

Last week, our Outreach Team visited The Pine School for a voter education presentation and registration drive.

Did you know? In Florida, eligible students can pre-register to vote at ages 16 or 17. Upon turning 18 years of age, students can vote by mail or in person.

It’s never too early to learn about the voting process, to be informed, and to be prepared to make your voice heard!

Today is the final day to register to vote before the November 3 General Election.

If you are not registered to vote, today is your last chance to make sure your voice is heard. The Martin County Elections Office will host voter registration drives from 10 a.m. to 1 p.m. at the Robert Morgade Library and Blake Library.

Citizens can register to vote, update their voter information, or request a vote-by-mail ballot.

The deadline to register to vote for the upcoming General Election is Monday, October 5.

Not registered yet? Need to update your voter record? Take a few minutes today to make sure your information is up to date and you’re ready to vote.

Visit MartinVotes.gov for more information or register online at RegisterToVoteFlorida.gov.

Don’t miss the deadline. Make your voice heard this election.
