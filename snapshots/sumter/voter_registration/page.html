How to Register to Vote

Listed here are some of the options voters have to register to vote in our county.

Please Note : New registration applications cannot be emailed or faxed. For all new registrations, we must receive the original form that contains the applicant's signature.

For additional information, please visit the Florida Division of Elections Voter Registration page.

Online

The Florida Department of State offers a secure and convenient voter registration option for Floridians to electronically register to vote and make updates to their existing record.

Please Note : To complete the online form, you will need the information from your Florida driver's license or a state identification card and the last four digits of your Social Security number.

If you do not have a Florida driver's license, Florida identification card, or social security number, you can still use the website to complete your application. However, you will have to print, sign, and deliver your application by mail or in-person to your county Supervisor of Elections office.

By Mail

You can download the state voter registration form and deliver your completed form either in person or by mail (address listed at the bottom of this page).
