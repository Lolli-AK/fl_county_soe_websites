Early Voting
Early Voting in Jackson County is open to all eligible registered voters. Early Voting lets you avoid long lines, and vote when and where it is most convenient for you.
When is Early Voting Available?
Early Voting is available as part of each election here in Jackson County. State law sets a minimum of eight days of Early Voting, but here in Jackson County we offer additional days so that voters have more flexibility when casting their ballot.
The Early Voting dates in Jackson County for 2026 are:
Primary Election
: August 6 – August 15, 8 AM – 5:30 PM daily
General Election
: October 22 – October 31, 8 AM – 5:30 PM daily
Where Can I Vote Early?
There are 3 Early Voting sites in Jackson County. Voters can vote at any Early Voting site in the county during voting hours.
Supervisor of Elections Office
Address: 2851 Jefferson St. Marianna, Fl 32448
Hours of Operation: 8am – 5:30pm
Graceville City Hall
Address: 5348 Cliff St. Graceville, Fl 32440
Hours of Operation: 8am – 5:30pm
Sneads City Hall
Address: 2028 Third Ave. Sneads, Fl 32460
Hours of Operation: 8am – 5:30pm
Who Can Vote Early?
Early Voting is open to all registered voters in Jackson County.
If you are registered to vote in another Florida county, but you have moved to Jackson County, we can update your address at the Early Voting site.
Do I Need ID to Vote Early?
You must have ID to vote at an Early Voting site
Can Anyone Enter an Early Voting Site?
The only people inside the Early Voting site during voting hours include:
Voters who are voting
Poll workers
Persons under the care of a voter, such as a child
Persons helping a voter with a disability
Official poll watchers
Supervisor of Elections staff
Law enforcement/emergency service personnel, if requested by the clerk
Can I Drop-Off my Vote-by-Mail Ballot at an Early Voting Site?
Yes, you can drop-off a Jackson County Vote-by-Mail ballot at any Early Voting site in the county during voting hours. Each Early Voting site has a secure ballot intake station (f/k/a drop box) for Vote-by-Mail ballots. For further information on secure ballot intake stations, please visit our
Secure Ballot Intake Station page
.
