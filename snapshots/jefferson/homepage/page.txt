🗳️ It’s Saturday, Jefferson County!
As you’re making your plans for the day, why not add Early Voting to the list?
Stop by the Jefferson County Supervisor of Elections Office and cast your ballot, then take some time to enjoy our community—grab a bite to eat, visit one of our wonderful local shops, and support the businesses that make Jefferson County such a special place to call home. ❤️
📍 Early Voting – Jefferson County Supervisor of Elections Office
🗓️ Today and daily through August 15
⏰ 8:00 a.m. – 5:00 p.m.
Make a plan. Cast your vote. Then enjoy your Saturday in Jefferson County!
