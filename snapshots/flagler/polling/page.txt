Find Your Precinct
There are 21 voting precincts in Flagler County. You MUST VOTE in the precinct where you live on Election Day.
Use this Precinct Finder to look up your voting location.
Directions for use:
STEP 1:
Enter your house number.
STEP 2:
As you type in your street name, matches will appear beneath the box. Click on your street name when it appears.
You do not need to enter the direction or street type. Example: If you live on East Kennedy Blvd., type in Kennedy
Please note you cannot copy and paste the address. It must be typed in.
