Taylor County, Florida
Supervisor of Elections
Dana Southerland is a lifelong resident of Taylor County and is married with three grown children. She was elected in August 2008 to serve as Supervisor of Elections for Taylor County and is currently serving her fifth term. Prior to being elected she worked in the elections office and the Clerk of Court. She has over thirty years of employment with Taylor County Government.
