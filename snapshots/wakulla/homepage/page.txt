Vote By Mail Ballot Quick Information
Can I pick up a Vote by Mail ballot from the office?
Yes, and your valid photo ID is required.  Once Early Voting begins, however, you will be required to attest that you are picking up a ballot due to an emergency.
Can I pick up a Vote by Mail ballot for another person?
Yes, but you must have written authorization from that person and present your valid photo ID.
You can pick up a ballot for an immediate family member (i.e., spouse, parent, child, grandparent, grandchild, or sibling, or the parent, child, grandparent, grandchild, or sibling of the voter’s spouse). If you want to pick up a ballot for a non-family member, please contact us.
The voter needs to have a Vote by Mail ballot request already on file.  If they don’t, they can submit the request form as part of the pickup.  So both the request form and the form designating a person to pick up their ballot must be submitted.
For more information and a link to the forms noted above, click the Vote by Mail icon above or contact us!

Florida Driver License Changes: What to Know
As of July 1, 2024, new Florida Driver License or state ID cards are issued new numbers.
Why is that important?
Your driver license is one of they ways we verify voters when they vote in person, by mail or submit a petition.
What should you do?
When renewing or updating your driver license, select the option to update your voter record.  When you do that, your voter record will be updated with your new number.
Didn’t opt to update?  What now?
Download the voter registration form below or call us at 850.926.7575 for assistance.
