Boys and Girls Club Election
On Friday, Boys & Girls Club members at the Greater Stuart Club participated in their very first mock election! Students experienced the full voting process—casting official ballots for Club President, Art Ambassador, four amendments, and a pizza referendum that was decided by just a few votes. They voted in real booths provided by the Supervisor of Elections and proudly wore their 'I Voted' stickers.
This hands-on experience gave students a firsthand look at how elections work. Supervisor of Elections, Vicki Davis, and her team were on site to oversee the process and tally the votes. Congratulations to all of the winners!
