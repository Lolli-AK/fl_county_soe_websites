Background Information

Early voting was approved by the Florida Legislature in 2004. Statewide, early voting is currently held a minimum of the 10th day through the 3rd day prior to any federal, state or county election, with the Supervisor of Elections having the option to provide early voting on the 15th, 14th, 13th, 12th, 11th and 2nd days prior to the election.

Early voting is offered during county-wide elections. Municipal elections not held in conjunction with county-wide elections (such as the March Municipal Elections and those municipalities that have November elections in odd years) will not have early voting.
