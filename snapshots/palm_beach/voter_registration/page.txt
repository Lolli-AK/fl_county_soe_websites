Register to Vote
If you are not already registered to vote in Florida, eligible individuals can register to vote at any time. The deadline to register to participate in an upcoming election is 29 days before the election.
Update Voter Information
Once you have registered to vote in Florida, you do not need to register again. However, you do need to keep your information up-to-date.
Restoration of Voting Rights
Learn about voting rights restoration, eligibility requirements, and available resources to help determine voter eligibility status.
