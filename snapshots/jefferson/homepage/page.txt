🗳️ **YOUR SAMPLE BALLOT IS READY!**
Want to see what will be on **YOUR ballot** for the November 3, 2026 General Election?
You can now view your personalized Sample Ballot online!
Because Jefferson County has different districts and ballot styles, **not every voter will have the exact same contests on their
ballot.**
The easiest way to make sure you're viewing the ballot that applies to you is to look up your individual voter information.
💻 Visit **
JeffersonVotesFL.gov**
➡️ Select **“Sample Ballot”** or **“Check Voter Status”**
➡️ Enter your information
➡️ View **your Sample Ballot**
Take a few minutes to review your ballot before you vote. Knowing what to expect ahead of time can make your voting experience easier and gives you time to become familiar with the contests and questions that will appear on your ballot.
📅 **General Election — November 3, 2026**
Questions? Give our office a call at **850-997-3348**. We’re happy to help!
