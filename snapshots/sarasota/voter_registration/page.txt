Register to Vote
It's quick and easy!
In Florida, the deadline to register for an upcoming election is
29 days
before that election. Sarasota County residents may apply to register to vote or update their existing voter registration in a variety of ways.
Online at
RegisterToVoteFlorida.gov
In person at any of our
three locations
By mail by completing the Florida Voter Registration Application:
English (PDF)
Español (PDF)
Mailing Address
:
Sarasota County Supervisor of Elections
P.O. Box 4194
Sarasota, FL 34230-4194
You may also hand-deliver the application to any supervisor of elections office in the state, a
driver license office
, a
voter registration agency
or armed forces recruitment office, or to the
Division of Elections
.
If your application is complete and you qualify as a voter, the supervisor of elections will mail you a voter information card as official confirmation that you are registered to vote. Make sure the information on your card is correct. If you do not receive confirmation within two weeks, or if you have any questions, call our Voter Services team 941.861.8619.
Who Can Register to Vote
To register to vote in Florida, you must:
Be a citizen of the United States of America
Be a Florida resident
Be 18 years old
Not now be adjudicated mentally incapacitated with respect to voting in Florida or any other state
Not have been convicted of a felony without your right to vote having been restored
If you are a member of the armed services or a military spouse or family member living away from your voting residence or a U.S. citizen living outside the United States, visit our
Military and Overseas Voters page
for more information.
Update Your Voter Record
Keeping your voter information current is an important part of the voting process. You can update your voter information online at
RegisterToVoteFlorida.gov
or by calling or visiting one of our
three office locations
. Visit our
Update Your Voter Information
page to learn more.
Preregister to Vote
A person who is otherwise qualified may preregister on or after his/her 16th birthday and may vote in any election on or after his/her 18th birthday. Once preregistered, a person may work with the elections office as a poll worker. Learn more about becoming a
student poll worker
.
Questions? Please call 941.861.8619.
