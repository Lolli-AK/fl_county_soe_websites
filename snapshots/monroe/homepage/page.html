2026 Primary Election Day Precinct Results Will Be Reported Later Than Usual

Monroe County voters should expect Election Day precinct results to be released later than in recent elections, Supervisor of Elections Sherri Hodies announced Friday. The delay applies only to ballots cast at precincts on Election Day. Early Voting and Vote-by-Mail results will be available by 7:30 p.m. on election night. For information, click below:

Welcome

We are happy to welcome all visitors to our website, especially those of you who are Monroe County voters. This site is primarily for you.

If you have arrived here - at our new site - by entering our old site address or through an old bookmark, please update your bookmarks to reflect our new site address: www.votemonroeflkeys.gov

Inside you'll find important information about our offices, how to register to vote , the voting process , and much more.
