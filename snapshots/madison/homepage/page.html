Madison County, Florida

Supervisor of Elections

Our goal is to keep you, “the public,” informed regarding changes in election laws and to ensure that all eligible residents register and vote! The right to vote is one of the most fundamental rights we enjoy as Americans and is also one of our greatest civic responsibilities. Make freedom count. Please register and vote! We offer several convenient ways to exercise your rights.
