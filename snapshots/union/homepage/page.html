Union County, Florida

Supervisor of Elections

With over 37 years of dedicated service to our local government, I’ve made it my life’s work to serve the people of Union County, Florida, with honesty and integrity.

I began my public service with the Union County Supervisor of Elections office in June 1988, where I served as Assistant Supervisor of Elections for over 19 years.  I was appointed as Supervisor of Elections for Union County in November 2007 to finish the term of the present Supervisor of Elections due to her death. I was elected by the voters of Union County in 2008 and have since served.
