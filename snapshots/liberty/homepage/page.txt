Your voice
Your vote
Thank you for visiting the website for the Liberty County Supervisor of Elections. This website will provide the most up-to-date information to make your voting experience as easy and convenient as possible because that experience is important to us! On this website you will find tools to help you get registered to vote; make updates to your registration; find candidate information; view election results; and more.
It is my goal as Supervisor to conduct fair and honest elections with transparency. My staff and I are dedicated to providing excellent customer service. Please do not hesitate to give us a call at
(850) 643-5226
. We are here to serve you!
Grant Conyers, Supervisor of Elections
