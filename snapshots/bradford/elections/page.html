2026 General Election

2026 Qualifying Dates:

Federal, Judicial, State Attorney & Public Defender Candidates:
Petition Deadline – March 23, 2026 at 12:00 noon.
Qualifying Week – April 20, 2026 at 12:00 noon thru April 24, 2026 at 12:00 noon.

Statewide, Multi-County, County and District Candidates:
Petition Deadline – May 11, 2026 at 12:00 noon.
Qualifying Week – June 8, 2026 at 12:00 noon thru June 12, 2026 at 12:00 noon.

Upcoming Municipal Elections

City of Starke:

Election Day – August 19, 2025
Early Voting – August 11 – 15, 2025 8am – 7pm at SOE Office.
Book Closing – July 21, 2025

City of Lawtey:

Election Day – December 2, 2025
Candidate Qualifying – September 22-26, 2025
Book Closing – November 3, 2025

City of Hampton:

The City of Hampton’s elections take place at the same time as the County General Elections. As such, their qualifying dates, book closing, vote by mail dates and election dates are the same as County elections. For more information, contact Hampton City Hall at (352) 468-1201

Town of Brooker:

The Town of Brooker will be holding their next election in 2027, with Qualifying to begin in December 2026.
