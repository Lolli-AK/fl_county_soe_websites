Important Information

  1. Current Election Statistics

    View a breakdown of ballots cast for an upcoming election.

    On election night, Tuesday, August 18, we will release and post preliminary, unofficial local election results shortly after 7:00 p.m. The 1st unofficial results, 2nd unofficial results (only for recounts), and official results will be compiled on the dates designated by the Canvassing Board Meetings and Logic and Accuracy Testing Schedule.

  2. Returning a Vote-by-Mail Ballot

    Vote-by-Mail ballots may be returned to any Supervisor of Elections office during regular business hours, or until 7:00 pm on Election Day . ( Office Locations )

    You may NOT return your Vote-by-Mail ballot to your polling place on Election Day. However, you MAY surrender your ballot and vote in person.

    Find out more information on returning your Vote-by-Mail ballot.

  3. Countdown to the Primary

    The first election of the season is the August 18 Primary Election. In our new video series, "Countdown to the Primary," Supervisor Persons-Mulicka shares important information to get you election-ready.

    As a nod to Election Day, new videos within the series will be posted on Tuesdays!

    Countdown to the Primary Video Series

  4. Congressional Redistricting

    In 2026, the Florida Legislature passed House Bill 1D, which establishes new or revised congressional districts in several counties across the state. While Lee County’s voters remain in either Congressional District 17 or 19, your congressional district may have changed from 17 to 19 or vice versa under the new legislative map. Some voters will not experience a change in their congressional district.

    View your personalized voting information, including your Congressional District, here.

  5. Vote-by-Mail Request Renewal Required

    The deadline to request a Vote-by-Mail ballot for the August 18, 2026, Primary Election was 5:00 pm, August 6.

    Under Florida law, all previous Vote-by-Mail requests have expired. You must submit a new request to vote by mail in the 2026 election cycle.

  6. Florida is a Closed Primary State

    The deadline to register to vote or change your party affiliation for the August 18, 2026, Primary Election was Monday, July 20.

    When registering to vote or changing your party affiliation, it is important to know that Florida is a closed primary state. This means that, in a Primary Election, voters not registered with a major political party may not have any candidates to vote for. However, they can vote on non-partisan candidates or issues that may appear on their ballot.

    Voters wishing to register to vote or change their party affiliation for a Primary Election must do so 29 days before that election.

    For more information on changing your political party affiliation for future elections, visit our Voter Registration Information page .

  7. Updating your Florida Driver's License or ID Card Number

    Have you recently updated or been issued a new Florida driver's license or ID card? Due to changes implemented to the numbering system by the Department of Highway Safety and Motor Vehicles (DHSMV) , your new driver's license or identification card number may be different from the one on your voter registration record. I t’s important to update your voter registration information with your new number. This identification number helps us verify your identity when processing your request for a vote-by-mail ballot or an address change.

    To update your voter registration with your new Florida driver’s license or ID card number, you may:

    • Call us at (239) LEE-VOTE (533-8683)
    • Visit RegisterToVoteFlorida.gov to update your record.
    • Complete and submit a Florida Voter Registration Application
      • Florida Voter Registration Application (English PDF)
      • Florida Voter Registration Application (Spanish PDF)
    • Update your registration when you update your driver’s license or ID card at the DMV.

  9. Online Service Tools

    If you need assistance, you have the option to use our online service tools, which are available on this site and do not require an in-person visit to our office. On this site, you can register to vote , update your voter registration record , make or update a Vote-by-Mail ballot request , or contact us .

    Please call the office at (239) LEE-VOTE (533-8683) if you require information that is not available on this site or if you need assistance using one of our online service tools.
