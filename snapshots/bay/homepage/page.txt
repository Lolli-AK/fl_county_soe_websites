🗳️ DON’T FORGET TO RETURN YOUR VOTE-BY-MAIL BALLOT!
Haven’t returned your Vote-by-Mail ballot yet? Don’t wait!
Return your ballot as soon as possible by mail or drop it off at the Bay County Supervisor of Elections Office.
⏰ Important: Your Vote-by-Mail ballot must be received by the Supervisor of Elections Office no later than 7:00 p.m. on Election Night in order to be counted.
Make your voice heard. Return your ballot today!

Accuracy in Elections and Voter Services is our Commitment to Excellence
The Bay County Supervisor of Elections is committed to maintaining the highest standards of quality in the planning, preparation, and execution of all electoral processes. We ensure that elections are fair, transparent, and conducted in accordance with legal requirements, fostering trust and confidence within our community.
