Vote Early
Early Voting is a convenient voting option available to all registered voters to cast a ballot in person prior to Election Day at any open Early Voting location designated by the Supervisor of Elections.
Early Voting is only required for elections containing a state or federal office and begins approximately 10 to 15 days before a county-wide election and ends 3 days before the election. Dates, times, and locations vary by county.
Vote-by-mail ballots must be received by the Supervisor of Elections office by 7 pm. Election Day to be counted and may be returned to a Secure Ballot Intake Station located at each early voting location during early voting hours, or in person to the Supervisor of Elections office located at 981 NE 16th Street, Ocala, FL 34470. (
Note:
Due to SB 90 Election Law changes, the 24/7 Ballot Intake Station previously located outside of the Supervisor of Elections office will no longer be available. However, voters may still utilize the Ballot Intake Station inside the Elections Office during business hours, Monday through Friday, 8:30 am to 4:30 pm.)
