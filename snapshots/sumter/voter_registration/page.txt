How to Register to Vote
Listed here are some of the options voters have to register to vote in our county.
Please Note
: New registration applications cannot be emailed or faxed. For all
new
registrations, we must receive the original form that contains the applicant's signature.
For additional information, please visit the
Florida Division of Elections Voter Registration
page.
Online
The Florida Department of State offers a secure and convenient voter registration option for Floridians to
electronically register to vote
and make updates to their existing record.
Please Note
: To complete the online form, you will need the information from your Florida driver's license or a state identification card and the last four digits of your Social Security number.
If you do not have a Florida driver's license, Florida identification card, or social security number, you can still use the website to complete your application. However, you will have to print, sign, and deliver your application by mail or in-person to your county Supervisor of Elections office.
By Mail
You can download the state voter registration form and deliver your completed form either in person or by mail (address listed at the bottom of this page).
State Voter Registration Form (English) (PDF)
State Voter Registration Form (Spanish) (PDF)
In Person
Visit our Wildwood or Bushnell Office location to complete your voter registration application.
Wildwood Office
Address: 7375 Powell Road
Suite #125
Wildwood, FL 34785
Bushnell Office
Address: 332 Dr. Martin Luther King Jr. Ave.
Bushnell, FL 33513
For more information on additional agencies or to request an application to be mailed to you, please contact us at 352-569-1540. Select 1 for List of Departments, then 1 for the Voter Service Department.
