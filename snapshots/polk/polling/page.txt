Where Do I Vote
The precinct you live in determines which races will be on your ballot and where you vote on Election Day. Use the precinct finder to find your precinct number, information about your precinct, and the office holders who represent voters in your precinct. You can also click on the link in the search tool to see a list of all precincts, and their current voting locations.
Florida law requires that you vote in the precinct where you live. Your voter information card shows the precinct, polling place, and voting district for your address.  If you move within the county, you must vote in your new precinct.  If you have any questions, please call (863) 534-5888.
