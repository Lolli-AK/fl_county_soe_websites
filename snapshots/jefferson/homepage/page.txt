🇺🇸 IT’S OFFICIAL! 🇺🇸
This morning, Monday, November 16, 2026, the Jefferson County Canvassing Board met and officially certified and submitted Jefferson County’s Official Results for the 2026 General Election to the Division of Elections.
We extend our sincere appreciation to our Canvassing Board members, election staff, poll workers and, of course, the voters of Jefferson County for their participation in this election.
Conducting an election requires months of preparation, countless hours of dedication and a tremendous team effort. We are proud to serve the citizens of Jefferson County and remain committed to conducting accurate, secure and transparent elections.
Official election results can be viewed on our website at
www.jeffersonvotesfl.gov
.
Thank you, Jefferson County!
