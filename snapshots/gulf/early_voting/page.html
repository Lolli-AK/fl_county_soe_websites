Vote Early

What to Expect

For what to expect at the polls, visit our Vote on Election Day page .

Required Identification

Any voter in the county may choose to cast their ballot during the early voting period. Be sure to bring your valid photo and signature identification with you when you present yourself to vote. Any voter who does not provide the proper identification will be offered a Provisional Ballot .

Voting times are:

8:00 am – 5:00pm est.

7:00 am – 4:00 pm cst.
