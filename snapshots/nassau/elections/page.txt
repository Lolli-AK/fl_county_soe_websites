See below for information on elections for the City of Fernandina Beach, the Town of Callahan, and the Town of Hilliard.
City of Fernandina Beach
* The City of Fernandina Beach General Election may occur in August or November depending on whether there are more than two candidates running in the same seat. If more than two candidates are running in a seat; their names will appear on the August ballot. If only two candidates are running in a Seat, their names will appear on the November ballot.
