Vote Early

Early Voting provides the flexibility to vote when and where it's most convenient for you. Early Voting dates, times, and locations vary by county. As long as you are registered to vote in Collier County, you can vote at one of the Early Voting locations we have across the county during the set period of time in which we offer Early Voting.

For all Florida elections in which there is a state or federal office race, the Supervisor of Elections is required to conduct Early Voting.

When Early Voting is activated, wait times will be listed below to help determine the best location to cast your ballot early.

We encourage voters to have a plan when voting during Early Voting. Bring your marked sample ballot with you to help limit time spent in the voting booth, and know what voter ID is required when voting in person. Voters who do not provide an acceptable form of identification will be provided with a provisional ballot .
