Vote Early
Early Voting provides a more convenient alternative to traditional Election Day voting. Early Voting allows you to cast your ballot in person as you would on Election Day, but at a time, day, or location that may be more convenient. Early Voting sites may also be less busy than an Election Day polling location.
Cast a ballot at any early voting location regardless of where you live in Okaloosa County. On Election Day, you must vote in your
designated polling location
. Vote-by-Mail ballots can be delivered to designated secure ballot intake stations at either of our office locations.
