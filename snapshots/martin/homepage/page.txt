• Vote by Mail Ballots begin to go out in the Mail to UOCAVA Voters: September 19, 2026
• Vote by Mail Ballots begin to go out in the Mail to Domestic Voters: September 24, 2026
• Deadline to Register to Vote: October 5, 2026
• Deadline to Request Vote by Mail Ballot: October 22, 2026
• Early Voting: October 21 – 31, 2026
• Election Day: November 3, 2026
