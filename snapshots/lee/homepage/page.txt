Updating your Florida Driver's License or ID Card Number
Have you recently updated or been issued a new Florida driver's license or ID card? Due to changes implemented to the numbering system
by the Department of Highway Safety and Motor Vehicles (DHSMV)
, your new driver's license or identification card number may be different from the one on your voter registration record. I
t’s important to update your voter registration information with your new number. This identification number helps us verify your identity when processing your request for a vote-by-mail ballot or an address change.
To update your voter registration with your new Florida driver’s license or ID card number, you may:
Call us at (239) LEE-VOTE (533-8683)
Visit
RegisterToVoteFlorida.gov
to update your record.
Complete and submit a Florida Voter Registration Application
Florida Voter Registration Application (English PDF)
Florida Voter Registration Application (Spanish PDF)
Update your registration when you update your driver’s license or ID card at the DMV.
