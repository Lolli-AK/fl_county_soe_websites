  8. Our Voting System

    Sarasota County uses a state-certified paper-based system of digital precinct scanners and high-speed central count optical scanners for voting and tabulation.

  9. Precinct List & Maps

    Access information about each of the 98 precincts in Sarasota County.

  10. Primary Elections

    The primary election precedes the November General Election and is the nominating process by which the political parties choose their preferred candidates.

  11. Sample Ballots

    Voters may use their sample ballots to research races and questions before voting. The supervisor of elections mails sample ballots to voter households and makes sample ballots available online prior to each election.

  12. Voter Statistics

    Each month, the elections office provides a set of standard precinct and district-level reports for review.
