Where to Go for Early Voting

During Early Voting, voters who are registered to vote in Monroe County can vote at ANY of the five Early Voting locations for elections that contain state and/or federal races:

    • Monroe County Elections Office - 5200 College Rd. Key West
    • Monroe County Elections Office Branch – 10015 Overseas Hwy. (MM 52.5), Marathon
    • Monroe County Elections Office Branch - Murray Nelson Government Center, 102050 Overseas Hwy. #137 (MM 102), Key Largo
    • Big Pine Key Community Park - 31009 Atlantis Dr. - End of Sands Road (MM 31), Big Pine Key
    • Plantation Key Community Center (old courthouse) - 88820 Overseas Hwy., Plantation Key
