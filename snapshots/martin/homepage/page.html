Has your signature changed? Now is the perfect time to update your signature of record.

Vote‑by‑mail ballots will be mailed tomorrow to all registered voters who have requested a ballot for the November 3 general election. An updated signature keeps your voter record current and up to date. When you vote by mail, sign a candidate petition, or an initiative petition, your signature must be verified against your signature on file.

To update your signature, visit the Elections Office at 135 SE Martin Luther King Jr. Blvd., or call 772‑288‑5637 for more information.

Bringing the Constitution to Life!

In honor of Constitution Week, Bre and Vera from the Martin County Supervisor of Elections Office joined members of our community as readers during the Constitution Reading at the Blake Library!

Also joining the celebration was Stuart City Commissioner Eula Clarke, pictured alongside our team and fellow participants.

What a meaningful way to celebrate Constitution Week—coming together as a community to recognize the document that has helped shape our nation for generations.

Thank you to the Blake Library for hosting this special event and inviting us to be part of the celebration!
