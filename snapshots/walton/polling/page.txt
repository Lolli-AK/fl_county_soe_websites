Early Voting Locations, Dates, & Times
Early Voting provides a more convenient alternative to traditional Election Day voting. Early Voting allows you to cast your ballot in person just as you would on Election Day, but at a time, day, or location that may be more convenient. Early Voting locations may also be less busy than an Election Day polling place and help reduce traffic on Election Day. Any Walton County voter can vote at any Early Voting location in the county. Use the following link to find the closest location and voting times.

If you choose to cast your ballot on Election Day, you must go to your home precinct’s polling place to do so. It is against the law to vote in a precinct other than the one you currently live in. Be sure your address is correct and
update
it, if needed, before voting. Use these links to find your correct Election Day polling place. You may also find your polling place listed on your current
voter information card
.
City (Municipal) Election Polling Places:
For each respective city’s election, all eligible voters will vote at the designated location for that city listed below:
DeFuniak Springs
DeFuniak Springs Community Center
361 N 10th St
DeFuniak Springs, FL 32433
Freeport
Freeport City Community Center
(behind City Hall)
112 St Hwy 20 W
Freeport, FL 32439
Paxton
Paxton Agriculture Complex
21972 US Hwy 331 N
Paxton, FL 32538
Please Note: Cities are responsible for conducting their own elections and candidate qualifying, administered by the city clerk and certified by the members of the city canvassing board. The role of the Walton County Supervisor of Elections Office may be to provide services to the city to assist, if requested, including providing ballots and the list of eligible voters.
