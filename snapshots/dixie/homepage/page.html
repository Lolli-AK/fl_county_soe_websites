Meet the Staff

The Dixie County Supervisor of Elections Office strives to conduct fair, honest, and transparent elections in the most efficient and professional manner. We believe that every single vote matters. Our mission is to ensure the integrity of the electoral process and your vote, while enhancing public confidence through voter education. We hope you will find our website user-friendly, with answers to your questions. As always, if you can’t find what you are looking for, please feel free to contact our office at (352) 498-1216, e-mail: [email protected] or just stop by our office and we will be happy to assist you.
