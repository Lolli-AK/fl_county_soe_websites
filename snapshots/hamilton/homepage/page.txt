Supervisor of Elections
It is our commitment to uphold the integrity and transparency of the electoral process in Hamilton County. Our office strives to provide citizens with the information and resources needed to actively participate in the democratic process. Our goal is to ensure that every election is conducted with fairness and precision, and we are dedicated to continuously enhancing the voting experience for all.
