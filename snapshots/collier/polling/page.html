Find My Precinct

The precinct you live in determines which races will appear on your ballot and where you vote on Election Day. Use our Precinct Finder on this page to view your precinct and the address of your polling location.

On Election Day, the polls are open from 7 am to 7 pm.  Registered voters who vote on Election Day must cast their ballot at their assigned precinct.

Precinct Finder

Type in your house number and begin typing your street name below. Addresses in Collier County will begin to populate. There is no "submit" button; instead, select your residential street and zip code when it populates for information about your precinct.
