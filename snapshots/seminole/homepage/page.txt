Prepared Voters.
Trusted Elections.
Committed to accurate, secure and accessible elections for every voter.
Our mission is to ensure every eligible voter can participate with confidence in elections that are secure, transparent, and accessible. Under the leadership of Supervisor Amy Pennock, we are your source for trusted information, exceptional service, and unwavering commitment to election integrity.
Whether you choose to vote by mail, vote early, or vote on Election Day, Seminole County offers convenient, reliable, and voter-friendly options.
We take pride in keeping lines short, providing convenient early voting access, and supporting voters of all abilities with fully accessible voting equipment.
Our office is here to make sure every voter is prepared, every ballot is secure, and every voice is heard.
Become an Election Worker
Serve your community. Support democracy.
Election workers play a vital role in ensuring elections run smoothly, securely, and fairly. By joining our team, you’ll help voters navigate the process, assist at polling locations, and support the integrity of Seminole County elections. No prior experience is required, just a commitment to public service and a willingness to help.
