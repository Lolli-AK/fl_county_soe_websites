Welcome to St. Johns County

The St. Johns County Supervisor of Elections is entrusted with the administration of elections that are secure, transparent, and accurate. We are committed to protecting every citizen’s fundamental right to vote and to ensuring accessible, reliable information for informed participation in the electoral process. Guided by principles of accountability, transparency, and impartiality, we conduct elections with the highest standards of integrity. We recognize that free and fair elections are essential to our democracy, and we remain dedicated to ensuring that every St. Johns County voter has an equal opportunity to be heard in shaping the future of their Country.

Vicky Oakes
