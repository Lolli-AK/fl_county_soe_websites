Register to Vote
If you are eighteen (18) years old, a U.S. citizen, and a permanent resident of the State of Florida you may register to vote.
If you are sixteen (16) years old, you can pre-register and receive your voter information card in the mail after your 18th birthday.
You can register online at
https://registertovoteflorida.gov/
You can register to vote in person at the Supervisor of Elections office at:
135 SE Martin Luther King Jr. Blvd, Stuart, FL 34994
OR
When obtaining a driver’s license at any Martin County Tax Collector’s Office.
The
Tax Collector’s website
has complete details regarding locations and hours.
You can obtain a Voter Registration Application at many other locations in Martin County.
A
complete list
of where to obtain applications is available.
Once your registration has been processed, a Voter Information Card will be mailed to you.
You are not entitled to register to vote if you have been adjudicated mentally incapacitated with regard to voting in this or any other state and you have not had your right to vote restored pursuant to law, or, if you have been convicted of any felony by any court of record and you have not had your right to vote restored pursuant to law.

It’s National Voter Education Week!
Last week, our Outreach Team visited The Pine School for a voter education presentation and registration drive.
Did you know? In Florida, eligible students can pre-register to vote at ages 16 or 17. Upon turning 18 years of age, students can vote by mail or in person.
It’s never too early to learn about the voting process, to be informed, and to be prepared to make your voice heard!
