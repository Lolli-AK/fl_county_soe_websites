Early Voting
Florida Voters have three options regarding the way they can cast their ballot. In addition to the Vote-By-Mail option or voting at their polling location on Election Day, more and more Floridians are choosing to take advantage of the Early Voting option.
Early Voting allows voters to cast a ballot before election day. For each election, the Bradford County Supervisor of Elections will announce the early voting period, which will be at least 8 days in length.
In Bradford County, the only Early Voting site is in the North wing of the Bradford County Courthouse, located at:
945 North Temple Avenue
Starke, FL 32091
Please have a Photo ID ready for a quicker check-in process. We offer Electronic Check-in and Ballot-On-Demand technology to make your experience more efficient.
Early Voting Dates for 2026:
Primary Election:
August 3rd - 16th, 2026 from 9:00am - 6:00 pm
General Election:
October 19th - November 1st, 2026 from 9:00am - 6:00pm
