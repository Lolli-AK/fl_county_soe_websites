Register to Vote
How to Register
You may register at the Office of the Supervisor of Elections. You may also call the office at
(850) 997-3348
for a form to be mailed to you or pick up a form at any of the following locations:
WIC Office & Food Stamp Office
Division of Driver’s License
Library
Armed Forces Recruiting Office
Online Voter Registration Application
You can also register online. Please fill out the following form and read all of the directions carefully. Please mail or deliver the printed form to the following address:
Michelle Milligan – Supervisor of Elections
1175 W Washington Street
Monticello, FL 32344
Online Voter Registration Application
Is Your Voter Registration Card Current?
Make sure your voter registration information is up to date before the next election. Here are a few common reasons you may need to update your record:
Have you moved since the last election?
Florida law states that you must vote in the precinct where you live. Notify the elections office if you change your address.
Do you wish to change your party affiliation?
Party changes must be made 29 days prior to an election.
Has your name changed?
You need to fill out a new registration form so your correct name will appear in the precinct register.
