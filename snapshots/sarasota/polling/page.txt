Find My Precinct and Polling Place
The precinct you live in determines which contests will be on your ballot and where you vote on election day. Use the precinct finder below to determine your precinct number and polling place. Please note, a precinct is not the same as a polling location. Your polling location is the actual place where you go to vote on election day.
In the appropriate fields below, enter your House Number and then the Street Name. As you begin to type the street name, the program will auto-populate a list of streets in Sarasota County. Select your street from the options and your precinct information will be displayed.
