    • Be Election Ready

      Find all the information you need to ensure you are election ready.

      • Upcoming and Past Elections

        Take a look at scheduled elections coming up in 2026 and 2027, as well as past elections going back to 2025, with access to older election data.

      • Register to Vote

        Read the requirements to register to vote in Miami-Dade County, as well as how to update your voter information.

      • Voter Information

        Get personalized voter information, including your sample ballot and polling place. You can also request a vote-by-mail ballot, check its status and make changes to your voter information.

      • Three Ways to Vote

        Read about early voting, voting by mail and voting on Election Day.

      • Voter Toolkit

        Understand the voting process and be sure you have everything you need when you go vote.

      • Petition Amendments, Initiatives & Referendums

        Citizens can petition to propose state amendments, as well as Miami-Dade and municipal initiatives and referendums, to go up for a vote if certain requirements are met.

      • Voters with Special Needs

        Voters with disabilities have multiple voting options that meet Americans with Disabilities Act (ADA) standards.

      • Provisional Voting

        A provisional ballot shall be issued to a person who may not meet voter requirements. The ballot is then reviewed by the Canvassing Board.

      • Overseas and Military Voters

        Overseas and military voters who are Miami-Dade County residents can access resources to participate in local elections.

      • Restoration of Voting Rights

        If you are convicted of a felony, you lose the right to vote in Florida until your voting rights are restored.

    • Candidates & Campaigns

      Resources for anyone who wants to run for office as well as political campaigns.

      • Information for Candidates

        Valuable information for candidates and those looking to run for office, including County candidates’ qualifying documents and campaign finance reports.

      • Candidate Qualifying Handbook

        Read laws and details about running for a Miami-Dade County office.

      • Political Committees

        Learn everything you need to know about forming a political committee.

      • Poll Watchers

        Each political party and each candidate may have one poll watcher in each voting location or early voting area at any one time during an election.

    • Statistics & Historical Data

      Access election results, data and more election-related information.

      • Past Election Results

        Access past statistics and results of elections.

      • Current Precincts, Districts and Municipalities

        Get breakdowns of current voting precincts, districts and municipalities, including municipal codes, after 2023 reprecincting.

      • Voter Registration Statistics

        Review current and past voter registration data broken down by political party, precinct, demographics and more.

      • Redistricting

        Every 10 years, the Florida Legislature and certain local jurisdictions redraw districts from which Florida voters elect local, state and federal government officials.

      • Reprecincting

        After redistricting, the Supervisor of Elections updates the county’s voting precincts to conform the precinct boundaries to the new district boundaries.

      • Election Calendar Archive

        Access elections that have been scheduled from 2009 to 2024.

      • Sample Ballots Archive

        Access sample ballots from past elections from 2004 to 2024.
