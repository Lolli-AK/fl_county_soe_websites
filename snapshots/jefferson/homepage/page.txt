🇺🇸 **BE ELECTION READY — BRING YOUR ID!** 🇺🇸
Planning to vote in person during Early Voting or on Election Day? Don't forget to bring your identification!
✅ Your identification must be current and valid and include your photo and signature.
✅ If your photo ID does not contain your signature, you may present an additional form of identification that includes your signature.
📋 Not sure what forms of ID are acceptable? We've got you covered! Check out the graphic below for the complete list.
