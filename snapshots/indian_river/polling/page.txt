Election Day Voting
Polls will be open on Election Day from 7 am to 7 pm, local time.
Be sure the address on record with the Elections Office is your current address. If your address differs, you may
update your voter information online
, or call the Elections Office at (772) 226-4700.
Voted mail ballots will not be accepted at polling locations on Election Day.
Find My Precinct
Enter your house number and street name in the appropriate fields below to locate your Election Day polling place.
Directions for use:
Enter your house number
Slowly type in your street name
You do not need to enter the direction or street type. (Example: If you live on East Kennedy Blvd., type in Kennedy)
Select your street name from the options that appear
*Please note you cannot copy and paste the address. It must be slowly typed in.*
