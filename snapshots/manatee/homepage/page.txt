Who Can Vote?
The deadline to register to vote in this election is October 5, 2026
All registered voters in Manatee County are eligible to vote in a General Election and may choose any candidate, regardless of party affiliation.
Three Ways to Vote
Vote by Mail
Voted ballots must be in the Manatee County Supervisor of Elections Office by 7 PM on November 3, 2026.
You must
request a Vote By Mail ballot
to receive one. Visit
this page
for more information about Vote By Mail
.
Early Voting

8:30 am to 6:00 pm daily

Election Day

To vote in person on Election Day, you must vote in the polling place assigned to your precinct.

Dates & Deadlines
October 5, 2026 – Last day to register to vote
October 22, 2026 – Last day to request a Vote-by-Mail ballot
November 3, 2026 – Election Day: Polls open 7am to 7pm
November 5, 2026 – 5pm Deadline to for Provisional Documentation or VBM Cure affidavit
Canvassing Board Schedule
October 14, 2026 – 9am Canvassing board meeting: Logic and Accuracy test of the tabulating equipment, reporting software, and automated independent audit system to be used during the 2026 General Election. Immediately following, canvass of Vote By Mail Ballots
October 21, 2026 – 9am Canvassing board meeting: Canvassing of Vote by Mail Ballots
October 27, 2026 – 9am Canvassing board meeting: Canvassing of Vote by Mail Ballots
November 2, 2026 – 9am Canvassing board meeting: Canvassing of Vote by Mail Ballots
November 3, 2026 – Election Day: Canvassing board will convene at 3pm
November 5, 2026 – 2pm Canvassing board meeting: Canvass of Provisional Ballots / Canvass Vote By Mail Ballots
November 13, 2026 – 4pm Canvassing board meeting: Canvassing of Overseas Ballots / Certification of Election Results
November 20, 2026 – 9am Post-Election Certification Automated Independent Audit
