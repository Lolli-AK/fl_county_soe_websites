Vote-by-Mail

The deadline to request a vote-by-mail ballot to be mailed for this election is August 6 at 5 PM. To request a vote-by-mail ballot, click here .


Vote-by-mail ballots must be received by the Supervisor of Elections Office by 7 PM on Election Day, August 18, 2026, to be counted.


Vote-by-mail ballots can be returned:

  • By mail
  • Secure Ballot Intake Stations, available at all early voting locations during the hours of voting
  • In-person at the Elections Office


Vote-by-mail ballots cannot be returned to Election Day precincts!
