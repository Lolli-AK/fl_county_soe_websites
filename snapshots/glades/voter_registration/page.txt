Registration Deadlines
Registration books close 29 days before any election. All new registration applicants and party change requests must be received by a voter registration agency or postmarked on or before that date to be eligible to vote in the election.
Since January 1, 2006 Florida has had a state-wide voter registration system. Moving between counties in Florida is now considered an address change and not subject to the 29-day book closing, which applies to persons registering for the first time in Florida.
The 29-day book closing requirement still applies to party changes
.
Voter Registration Book Closing Dates for 2026:
Primary Election
: Books close July 20, 2026.
General Election
: Books close October 5, 2026.
