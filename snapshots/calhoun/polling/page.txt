Special Note
A precinct number followed by the letter “C” indicates that the precinct is city or town voters. For example, “Precinct 101” indicates that the precinct is county voters only, and “Precinct 101C” indicates the precinct is city voters only.
MUNICIPAL PRECINCTS LOCATED IN CALHOUN COUNTY:
City of Blountstown: Precincts 101C, 401C and 501C
Town of Altha: Precinct 201C
COUNTY DISTRICT / PRECINCT ASSIGNMENTS:
District 1: Precincts 101, 101C & 102
District 2: Precincts 201, 201C & 202
District 3: Precincts 301 & 302
District 4: Precincts 401, 401C
District 5: Precincts 501, 501C, 502 & 503
FLORIDA LEGISLATURE DISTRICT / PRECINCT ASSIGNMENTS:
All Calhoun County districts/precincts are located in Florida House of Representatives District 5.
All Calhoun County districts/precincts are located in Florida Senate District 2.
