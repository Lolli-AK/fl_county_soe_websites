Precinct Finder
Use this Precinct Finder to view your current
voting precinct and polling place
. You may also view your
precinct-specific sample ballot
and other election-related information for the next election (when available).
Enter your house number and street name in the appropriate fields below to locate your Election Day polling place.
Directions for use:
Enter your house number
Type in your street name
You do not need to enter the direction or street type. Example: If you live on East Bay Drive, type in
Bay.
Select your street name from the list that appears
Please note you cannot copy and paste the address. It must be typed in.
