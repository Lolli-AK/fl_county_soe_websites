WELCOME!
It is our goal to provide you with statistics, links to related election sites, answer questions, make available forms necessary to register to vote, request vote-by-mail ballots, poll worker applications, and as much historical information as possible.
Thank you for taking the time to visit the website. It is a pleasure to provide you with the information you need to be an active participant in the elections process.
The right to vote is one of the most fundamental liberties we enjoy as Americans and it is also one of our greatest civic responsibilities. Make freedom count. Register and vote.
Tappie A. Villane, Supervisor of Elections Santa Rosa County

County Judge Seat 3
On April 16, 2026, Judge Matt Gordon submitted his resignation letter from the First Judicial Circuit, County Judge Seat 3 effective April 17, 2026. Governor DeSantis accepted his resignation, and pursuant to Article V, Section 11(b) of the Florida Constitution, the Governor will now fill by appointment the vacancy created by this resignation, and the position is no longer up for election during the 2026 election cycle. The pre-filed candidates listed on the Candidate page will now file Termination Reports. As a result, that contest will not appear on the ballot in 2026.
