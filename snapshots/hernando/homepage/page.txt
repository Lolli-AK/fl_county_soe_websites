Sample Ballots Now Available!
Sample ballots for the 2026 General Election are now available.
Check the
Voter Lookup Tool
to view your specific sample ballot for your precinct.
You can view all of the sample ballots for the 2026 General Election below or by visiting
2026 Elections Information
.
General Election - November 3, 2026 - State Representative District 52 Sample Ballot (PDF)
NOTE
: The State Representative District 52 contest will
ONLY
appear on ballots in precincts: 350, 450, 500, 510, 520, 530, 540, 550.
General Election - November 3, 2026 - State Representative District 53 Sample Ballot (PDF)
NOTE
: The State Representative District 53 contest will
ONLY
appear on ballots in precincts: 100, 110, 120, 130, 140, 150, 160, 200, 210, 220, 230, 240, 250, 300, 310, 320, 330, 340, 360, 400, 410, 420, 430, and 440.
General Election - November 3, 2026 - City of Brooksville Sample Ballot (PDF)
NOTE
: The City of Brooksville contests will
ONLY
appear on the ballot in precinct 510.
General Election - November 3, 2026 - Sterling Hill CDD Sample Ballot (PDF)
NOTE
: The Sterling Hill CDD contest will
ONLY
appear on ballots for voters whose precinct is within the Sterling Hill Community Development District.
Ballots for voters in precinct 440 that are not apart of the Sterling Hill CDD will not have that contest.
By reviewing your sample ballot, you can see the specific contests and candidates that will appear on your ballot.
