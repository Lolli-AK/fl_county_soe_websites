Jefferson County Supervisor of Elections - Michelle Milligan
posted 10 hours ago
🎬 THAT’S A WRAP — 2026 PRIMARY ELECTION! 🗳️🇺🇸
And just like that…the 2026 Primary Election is in the books!
What voters see on Election Day is really just the final chapter of a story that begins months before the polls ever open.
These pictures capture just a glimpse of what goes into preparing for and conducting an election. It starts with community outreach—getting out into Jefferson County, registering voters, sharing important election dates and voting information, answering questions, and encouraging citizens to be informed and ready to vote.
Behind the scenes, the work continues with laying out and proofing ballots, preparing and testing voting equipment, recruiting and training poll workers, packing supplies, preparing polling places, assisting voters, conducting Early Voting, Election Day, election night, canvassing…and so much more!
It truly takes a village to conduct an election.
We could not do it without our incredible Elections Team. Our staff works countless hours behind the scenes, taking care of hundreds of details—many that most people will never see—to make sure Jefferson County is ready when it’s time to vote. We are incredibly proud of them and grateful for their dedication. ❤️
And to our poll workers—THANK YOU! Some worked their very first election this year, while others stepped into new positions and took on new responsibilities. Whether it was your first election or one of many, you showed up, worked long hours and helped make Election Day possible. We appreciate each and every one of you!
We also recognize the candidates who put themselves forward to serve. Campaigning takes countless hours, hard work and dedication, and we appreciate everyone who participated in the election process.
And now for something Jefferson County should be VERY proud of…
🎉 Jefferson County had a 42.27% voter turnout, ranking
#4
in the State of Florida for the 2026 Primary Election! 🎉
That is certainly something worth celebrating, especially for a Midterm Primary Election!
But there’s still room to grow. At 42.27%, more than half of our registered voters did not cast a ballot.
So, Jefferson County…what do you say we do even better in November? 😉
There isn’t much time to rest around the Elections Office because the November 3rd General Election is right around the corner—which means it’s time to start the process all over again!
To our staff, poll workers, polling locations, Canvassing Board, candidates, voters, community partners and everyone who played a part along the way—THANK YOU!
From outreach to Election Day and everything in between, it truly takes a village, and we are grateful for ours. ❤️
🎬 2026 Primary Election…THAT’S A WRAP!
Next stop: November 3rd! 🇺🇸🗳️
