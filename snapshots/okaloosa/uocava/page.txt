Military & Overseas Voters
Who Qualifies Under UOCAVA
The Federal Uniformed and Overseas Citizens Absentee Voting Act (UOCAVA) and conforming state legislation cover voting rights for the following voters residing outside of their home county or overseas:
Active Duty members of the US Armed Forces, spouses, and dependents
The Merchant Marine, spouses, and dependents
US citizens residing overseas only
Voter Registration Options
Using the Federal Post Card Application (FPCA)
Use the Federal Post Card Application (FPCA) to register or update registration and request Vote by Mail ballots. Sources for the FPCA:
Available from the Voting Assistance Officer
Federal Voting Assistance Program at
fvap.gov
Using the Florida Voter Registration Application (FVRA)
Use the Florida Voter Registration Application (FVRA) or online voter registration to register or update registration. Military voters currently residing in their home county should use the FVRA.
FVRA sources:
All local libraries and Supervisor of Elections Offices
Vote Okaloosa Register to Vote Page
RegistertoVoteFlorida.gov
Note:
Online registration requires a Florida Driver’s License and the last four digits of your Social Security Number. If you do not have a Florida license, the application can be printed and mailed.
Request Vote-by-Mail Ballots
Request Vote-by-Mail Ballots by mail, email, fax, phone, or on this website.
Request A Vote-by-Mail Ballot
Status of Vote-by-Mail Ballot
Important Information for Overseas Voters
Your ballot must be received no later than 7:00 pm Election Night in a Primary Election; and must be postmarked or dated no later than Election Day, and received no more than ten (10) days, following a Presidential Preference Primary or General Election.
General Information
Request your Vote-by-Mail Ballot as early as possible. Don’t wait until the last few days before an election.
Please include an email address in any correspondence/application.
Vote-by-Mail requests will be effective for all elections through the next regularly scheduled General Election.
Please update your mailing address so undeliverable ballots do not jeopardize your active voter registration status.
Ballots can be delivered by mail, email, or accessed online at
EnhancedBallot.com
. Overseas voters may have ballots delivered by fax.
Ballots will be delivered at least 45 days prior to an election to voters with a request on file.
Voters in the US must return ballots by mail or in person. The ballot must be received by 7 pm on Election Day.
Overseas voters may return ballots by mail or fax. FVAP may be able to help return a ballot by fax. Email them at
[email protected]
. The ballot must be received by 7 pm Election Night for a Primary. For a Presidential Preference Primary or General Election, the ballot must be postmarked or dated no later than Election Day and received no more than ten (10) days following the election.
Do not return ballots by email!
Federal Write-In Absentee Ballot (FWAB)
The Federal Write-In Absentee Ballot (FWAB) is a back-up or emergency ballot. Use the FWAB if you are outside your voting jurisdiction and did not receive your requested regular ballot.
Before using the FWAB, please contact our office by email or phone and check ballot status. The FWAB may be used to vote in all races and referenda on the current ballot.
The FWAB is available online through the following websites or from the Voting Assistance Officer.
Federal Voting Assistance Program at
fvap.gov
Overseas Vote Foundation at
overseasvotefoundation.org
State Write-In Ballot
No earlier than 180 days but not later than 90 days before a General Election, an overseas voter may request a state write-in vote by mail ballot.
The voter shall state that due to military or other contingencies that preclude normal mail delivery, the voter cannot vote a regular vote by mail ballot during the normal vote by mail voting period.
The ballot will contain only the candidate races. The voter must fill in the candidate names or the political party names.
Please contact our office for details or alternatives to the State Write-In Ballot.
Late Registration
An individual or accompanying family member who is otherwise qualified and who, after the book closing date for an election:
has been discharged or separated from the uniform services or the  Merchant Marine
has returned from a military deployment or activation
has separated from employment outside the territorial limits of the US
may register to vote until 5 pm on the Friday before that election in the office of the Supervisor of Elections. Such persons must produce sufficient documentation showing evidence of qualifying for late registration.
