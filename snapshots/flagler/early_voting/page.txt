Early Voting
Vote Early in Flagler County
Recent turnout shows more voters are choosing to vote early!
Any registered and qualified voter who is eligible to vote in a countywide election may vote in person at an early voting site.
Voters who have requested a mail ballot may return their ballot at a secure ballot intake station at one of our early voting sites, during the schedule below. More information about secure ballot intake stations is available at the bottom of this page.
According to Florida law, every voter must present a Florida driver license, a Florida identification card, or another form of acceptable picture and signature identification in order to vote. If you do not present the required identification or if your eligibility cannot be determined, you will only be permitted to vote a provisional ballot.
Don't forget your ID!

Vote By Mail Ballot Return - Secure Ballot Intake Stations
Voted mail ballots may be returned by using one of our secure ballot intake stations during an election cycle. These secure ballot intake stations will be monitored and available at the Elections Office and all early voting sites during the early voting period. Voters may deposit their mail ballots at any time the early voting sites are open for voting.
You may choose to return your ballot directly to the Elections Office, or one of our early voting sites:
Elections Office Mail Ballot Return
Elections Office Drop Off
This secure ballot intake station is not available after office hours or on weekends, except during the early voting period. You return any election-related paperwork and voted mail ballots during an election cycle.
Elections Office Drop Off Schedule
Available during office hours and extended office hours until 6:00 PM during the early voting period.
Office hours:
Monday through Friday from 8:30 AM – 4:30 PM
Any person may not return more than two Vote-By-Mail ballots per election in addition to his or her own ballot, or a ballot belonging to an immediate family member. The term “immediate family” means a person’s spouse or the parent, child, grandparent, grandchild, or sibling of the person or the person’s spouse. Reference: Section 104.0616, F.S.
Early Voting Mail Ballot Return
Early Voting Drop Off
These secure ballot intake stations are available at all of our early voting sites during early voting hours. Any voter can return their mail ballot at an early voting site. The secure ballot intake stations are located in or near the lobby area at these locations. Ballots returned are delivered to the Elections Office the same day.
