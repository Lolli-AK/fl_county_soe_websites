Momentum Monday: What a Month!
As August comes to a close, we’re looking back on a busy and exciting month here at the Martin County Supervisor of Elections Office!
From preparing for the Primary Election and welcoming voters during Early Voting to working alongside our incredible Election Workers on Election Day, August was all about teamwork, preparation, and serving Martin County voters.
To our Election Workers, community partners, voters, and Elections Team — thank you for helping make this month a success!
We’re carrying that momentum right into September and looking forward to everything still ahead!

Making Waves in Martin County!
Our Elections Team traded the office view for some coastal scenery this morning as they represented the Martin County Supervisor of Elections Office at the Jensen Beach Chamber of Commerce Breakfast, hosted at the beautiful Florida Oceanographic Coastal Center in Stuart!
It was a fin-tastic morning spent catching up with familiar faces, meeting local businesses and community leaders, and enjoying one of Martin County’s coastal treasures!
We love seeing our team out and about representing the Martin County Supervisor of Elections Office throughout the community — and this morning, they certainly made a splash!
Thank you to the Jensen Beach Chamber of Commerce and Florida Oceanographic Coastal Center for a wonderful morning!

Meet Our Deputy of First Impressions and Office Morale, Finley!
In honor of National Dog Day, we’re shining the spotlight on one of the most paw-some members of our Elections Team — Finley!
As our official Deputy of First Impressions and Office Morale, Finley takes her responsibilities very seriously. From reporting for duty during the week to bringing smiles and tail wags throughout the office, she’s always ready to lend a paw and help us serve Martin County voters!
Her daily duties include:
▪Boosting office morale
▪Supervising important election business
▪Greeting the Elections Team with plenty of enthusiasm
▪Bringing joy to everyone she meets
▪Keeping a watchful eye on office operations
▪And, of course, making sure treat distribution is handled properly!
Finley is a dedicated Election Worker, although her compensation package looks a little different from ours — she happily accepts treats, belly rubs, and the occasional extra lunch!
We couldn’t imagine the Elections Center without our favorite four-legged coworker!
Happy National Dog Day from Finley and the Martin County Elections Team!
