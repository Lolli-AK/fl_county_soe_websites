Welcome
I encourage you to browse through the various features of our website. With the help of this site, we hope to encourage citizen participation, enhance public confidence in the election process, and to educate citizens of all ages. I hope that you find this site beneficial and most helpful with all election questions and election needs. It is our desire to bring you as much information as possible about the voting process in an effort to help good citizens become good voting citizens. This site is a work in progress so if you have any questions or suggestions concerning this website please let us know.
If I or my staff can be of any assistance to you, please feel free to call, e-mail, or visit the elections office at 32 N 5th St, Suite A. Our office hours are Monday - Friday 8:30am - 5:00pm
With gratitude,
Chris Milton
Baker County Supervisor of Elections
Official Notices
Logic & Accuracy Test
Pursuant to Florida Statutes 101.5612 the Logic and Accuracy Certification Board will conduct the logic and accuracy test of the automatic county equipment (for early voting, vote-by-mail, and for the equipment going to the polls) on July 29, 2026 at 9:00am. The test will be conducted for the August 18, 2026 Primary Election. The test will be held at the Baker County Supervisor of Elections Office, 32 N 5th Street, Macclenny, FL 32063.
